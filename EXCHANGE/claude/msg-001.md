## 2. Topic and decomposition

IDEA: **culinary formulation** — an expert that designs and adapts baking/cooking
recipes from first principles. AUDIENCE: home cooks who want engineering-grade answers.

Three subdomains (one KB each):

1. `kb1` — **ingredient chemistry**: what each ingredient contributes (water / fat /
   sugar / protein / solids), substitution equivalences, acid–leavening chemistry,
   what breaks when a component is removed (e.g. egg-free structure).
2. `kb2` — **formulation math**: baker's percentages, budget bands (hydration, fat,
   sugar, leavening as % of flour), pan geometry, scaling laws (e.g. bake time vs
   batter depth), closing a formula's budgets.
3. `kb3` — **technique & doneness protocol**: mixing methods and their order of
   operations, doneness signal hierarchy (internal temp dominates skewer/color),
   failure modes (sunk center, dense crumb) and recovery/prevention rules.

## 3. What to produce

Exactly four authored files under `incoming/culinary/`:

- `kb1.spec.json`, `kb2.spec.json`, `kb3.spec.json` — compact KB specs following
  `FACTORY/kb_spec.template.json`.
- `culinary.specialist.json` — following `FACTORY/specialist.template.json`.

Hard requirements (the gates enforce these — do not deviate):

- Count bands PER KB spec: nodes 19–24, edges 32–40, conflict_axes 8–10,
  edge_cases 10–12, workflow 9–12, competency_questions 10–14, dominance_rules 7–12,
  anti_rework_rules 7–12, iteration_protocol 6–10.
- Node ids UPPER_SNAKE and unique; `dependencies` ACYCLIC; every edge `from`/`to`
  and every `*_ref` points at a real id; a `conflict` edge MUST carry a
  `resolution_rule`; `risk_if_wrong >= 0.80` ⇒ non-empty `acceptance_tests`;
  `cross_topic_coupling >= 0.75` ⇒ non-empty `revisit_triggers`; all 15 base
  metrics are numbers in [0,1], plus `uncertainty_interval: [lo,hi]` and an
  `update_signal` string per node.
- Specialist: all 15 required keys present; `_directive` contains the word
  "machine"; every capability has a method AND a `when_to_use`; capabilities cover
  all 3 KBs; `grounded_in_kbs` lists the FORGED outputs
  (`incoming/culinary/kb1.kb.json` etc.) — paths that exist after you run the forge.
- NEVER emit the literal strings `TODO`, `placeholder`, `<...>`, `NODE_A`,
  `SHORT_ID` anywhere — the gate rejects them.
