# SK-03 · The road test

> **Handbook:** Section 13, pages 13-1–13-4 · **Test:** Skills Tests · **Exam weight:** Medium · **Study time:** ~40 min

## What you'll be able to answer
- The pass rule: **no more than 30 errors** and **no critical driving errors** (a critical error is an **automatic failure**).
- The basic rules for the whole drive: **safety belt**, obey every law, **no crash or moving violation**, English only.
- What the examiner watches for in **turns, intersections, lane changes, the expressway, the stop/start, curves and railroad crossings**.
- The special railroad crossing steps for **buses, school buses and placarded vehicles** (stop **15–50 feet** from the nearest rail).
- The **general driving behaviors** scored all the time: clutch, gears, brakes, lane use, steering, traffic checks and turn signals.

## Learn it

The road test is the **third** and last skills test. You drive a route with many kinds of traffic while the examiner scores you. The examiner scores **specific maneuvers** (like a turn or a lane change) and also your **general driving behavior** the whole time. You follow the examiner's directions, which are given early enough for you to do them. You will **never be asked to drive unsafely**. (p. 13-1)

The road test is one part of what the handbook calls the CDL **driving performance evaluation (DPE)**. (p. 13-1)

**Key words**
- **Error** = a scored mistake on the road test.
- **Critical driving error** = a serious mistake that ends the test as an **automatic failure**, no matter how few other errors you have.
- **Moving violation** = breaking a traffic law while driving, such as running a stop sign.
- **Simulate** = act out or explain what you would do, when the route does not have that situation.
- **Coasting** = moving with the vehicle out of gear (clutch pressed in or shift lever in Neutral).
- **Safe gap** = enough space behind a stopped vehicle that you can see its **rear tires** touching the road.
- **Placards** = the diamond-shaped signs on a vehicle carrying hazardous materials.
- **Double-clutch** = shifting by pressing and releasing the clutch twice, needed on an unsynchronized manual transmission (see p. 2-10).
- **Rev / lug the engine** = run the engine too fast (rev) or too slow and struggling (lug) for the gear you are in.
- **Ride the clutch / ride the brake** = keep your foot resting on the pedal so it is partly pressed.

### 13.1 How you will be tested (p. 13-1)
- **Pass rule:** make **no more than 30 errors** and **no critical driving errors**. A critical driving error = **automatic failure**. (p. 13-1)
- **No interpreters**; the whole road test is in **English**. Language warnings: **2** verbal warnings, the **3rd** offense on the same test date = **automatic failure**. (p. 13-1)
- **Backup cameras and self-parking** cannot be the **only** thing you rely on. (p. 13-1)
- The whole time you must: **wear your safety belt**; **obey all traffic signs, signals and laws**; and finish **without an accident or a moving violation**. (p. 13-1)
- If the route lacks a situation (for example, a railroad crossing), you may be asked to **simulate** it by **telling the examiner** what you would do. (pp. 13-1, 13-3)
- You have **3 attempts in total** to pass the vehicle inspection, basic control skills and road tests. (p. 13-1)
- To meet the Class B skills test rule, you must test in a truck or bus, or another **single vehicle** with a **GVWR of 26,001 lb or more**. (p. 13-1)

### 13.1.1 Turns (pp. 13-1–13-2)
**When told to turn:** check traffic **in all directions**, signal, and get into the right lane for the turn safely. (p. 13-1)

**Approaching the turn:**
- **Signal** to warn others. **Slow down smoothly** and change gears as needed to keep power.
- **Do not coast unsafely.** *Unsafe coasting* = being out of gear for **more than the length of your vehicle**. (p. 13-1)

**If you must stop first:**
- Stop **smoothly**, **without skidding**, and **fully**, **behind** the stop line, crosswalk or stop sign.
- Behind another vehicle, stop where you can **see its rear tires** (a safe gap).
- **Don't let the vehicle roll.** Keep the **front wheels pointed straight ahead**. (p. 13-1)

**Making the turn:**
- Check traffic **in all directions** again. Keep **both hands on the wheel**.
- Keep **checking your mirror** so the vehicle does not hit anything on the **inside** of the turn.
- Don't swing into **oncoming traffic**. **Finish in the correct lane.** (p. 13-1)

**After the turn:** make sure the **signal is off**; get up to the **speed of traffic**; signal and move to the **right-most lane** when safe (if not there already); **check your mirrors**. (p. 13-2)

### 13.1.2 Intersections (p. 13-2)
**Approaching:**
- Check traffic thoroughly **in all directions**. Slow down **gently**, brake **smoothly**, and change gears if needed.
- If you must stop, stop **completely** (no coasting) **behind** stop signs, signals, sidewalks or stop lines, with a **safe gap** behind the vehicle ahead. The vehicle must **not roll** forward or back.
- **Do not enter** the intersection if there is **not enough room to clear it**. *Why:* a long vehicle stuck in the middle blocks cross traffic. (p. 13-2)

**Driving through:** check all directions; slow down and **yield** to pedestrians and traffic in the intersection; **don't change lanes** in the intersection; keep your **hands on the wheel**. (p. 13-2)

**After:** keep checking your mirrors; speed up smoothly and shift as needed. (p. 13-2)

### 13.1.3–13.1.5 City streets, lane changes and the expressway (p. 13-2)
**Urban business area:** make **regular traffic checks**, keep a **safe following distance**, stay **centered** in the **right-most lane**, and keep up with traffic but **never exceed the posted speed limit**. (p. 13-2)

**Lane changes:** on multi-lane roads you will be told to change lanes **to the left, then back to the right**. For each one: **traffic checks first**, **signal**, **wait for a safe gap**, and keep a safe following distance. (p. 13-2)

**Expressway / highway** (p. 13-2):
| Stage | What the examiner wants |
|---|---|
| Entering | Check traffic, signal, **merge smoothly** into the proper lane |
| On the expressway | Right **lane position**, **spacing** and **speed**; keep checking traffic in all directions |
| Exiting | Traffic checks, signal, **slow down smoothly in the exit lane**; on the ramp keep slowing **inside the lane markings** with good spacing |

### 13.1.6 Stop/start (pp. 13-2–13-3)
You pull over **as if you were going to get out and check something** on the vehicle. Check traffic in all directions and move to the **right-most lane or shoulder**. (p. 13-2)

**Preparing to stop:** check traffic, turn on the **right** signal, slow down smoothly, brake evenly, shift as needed, and come to a **full stop without coasting**. (p. 13-2)

**Once stopped** (p. 13-3):
1. The vehicle is **parallel** to the curb or shoulder and **safely out of traffic**.
2. It is **not blocking** driveways, fire hydrants, intersections, signs, etc.
3. **Cancel** your turn signal.
4. Turn **on** your **4-way emergency flashers**.
5. **Set the parking brake**.
6. Shift to **Neutral or Park**.
7. Take your **feet off** the brake and clutch pedals.

**When told to go again** (p. 13-3):
1. Check your mirrors **in all directions**.
2. Turn **off** the 4-way flashers.
3. Turn on the **left** signal.
4. When traffic allows, **release the parking brake** and **pull straight ahead**. **Don't turn the wheel before the vehicle moves.**
5. Check traffic from all directions — **especially the left** — and steer and speed up smoothly into the lane when safe.
6. **Cancel** the left signal once you are back in traffic.

### 13.1.7–13.1.8 Curves and railroad crossings (p. 13-3)
**Curves:** check traffic, **slow down before the curve** so you **don't need to brake or shift in it**, stay in your lane, and keep checking traffic. (p. 13-3)

**Railroad crossing — every commercial driver** (p. 13-3):
- Slow down, brake smoothly, shift as needed.
- **Look and listen** for trains; check traffic in all directions.
- On a multi-lane road, stay in the **right-most lane**.
- **Don't stop, change gears, pass another vehicle, or change lanes** while **any part** of the vehicle is **in the crossing**.

**Extra steps — bus, school bus, or vehicle with placards** (at every crossing unless it is **exempt**) (p. 13-3):
1. Turn on the **4-way flashers** as you approach.
2. **Stop no more than 50 feet and no less than 15 feet** from the **nearest rail**.
3. **Listen and look both ways** along the track for a train and for signals. In a **bus**, you may also have to **open the window and door** before crossing.
4. Keep **both hands on the wheel** while crossing.
5. **Don't stop, change gears or change lanes** while any part of the vehicle is on the tracks.
6. Turn **off** the flashers **after** you cross. Keep checking your mirrors.

Not every route has a crossing; you may be asked to **explain and demonstrate** the steps at a **simulated** location. (p. 13-3)
- *Worked example:* you stop a placarded truck with the bumper 60 feet from the nearest rail — **too far**. At 10 feet — **too close**. Anywhere from 15 to 50 feet is right.

### 13.1.9–13.1.10 Bridges, overpasses, signs and student discharge (p. 13-4)
- After driving **under an overpass**, you may be asked the **posted clearance (height)**. After driving **over a bridge**, you may be asked the **posted weight limit**. *Why:* a CMV driver must know whether the vehicle fits under and is light enough for what is ahead. (p. 13-4)
- If there is no bridge or overpass, you may be asked about **any other traffic sign** on the route — be ready to **name and explain** it. (p. 13-4)
- **School bus endorsement:** you must **demonstrate loading and unloading students** (the steps are in Section 10). (p. 13-4)

### 13.1.11 General driving behaviors (p. 13-4)
These are scored for the **whole** drive:
| Category | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Clutch** (manual) | **Always use the clutch** to shift; **double-clutch** on an unsynchronized transmission | Rev or lug the engine; **ride the clutch** to control speed; coast with the clutch in; **"pop"** the clutch |
| **Gears** (manual) | Pick a gear that doesn't rev or lug the engine | **Grind or clash** gears; **shift in turns or intersections** |
| **Brakes** | Brake **smoothly** with **steady pressure** | **Ride or pump** the brake; brake **harshly** |
| **Lanes** | Stop **behind** stop lines, crosswalks or stop signs; stay in or move to the **right-most lane** unless it is blocked | Drive over **curbs, sidewalks or lane markings** |
(p. 13-4)

**Where to finish a turn on a multi-lane road** (p. 13-4):
- **Left turn:** end in the lane **directly to the right of the center line**.
- **Right turn:** end in the **right-most (curb) lane**.

### 13.1.12–13.1.14 Steering, traffic checks and turn signals (p. 13-4)
- **Steering:** don't **over-steer** or **under-steer**. Keep **both hands on the wheel at all times** except while shifting, then put both hands back right away. (p. 13-4)
- **Regular traffic checks:** check traffic and **mirrors regularly**; check mirrors **before, during and after** an intersection; **scan** in busy areas and where **pedestrians** are likely. (p. 13-4)
- **Turn signals:** use them **properly**, **when required**, and at the **right time**; **cancel** them after the turn or lane change. (p. 13-4)

## Exam traps
- **Trap:** You pass as long as you have 30 or fewer errors. → **Correct:** you also need **zero critical driving errors** — one critical error is an **automatic failure**. (p. 13-1)
- **Trap:** Rolling in Neutral up to a stop is fine if it's smooth. → **Correct:** being out of gear for **more than your vehicle's length** is **unsafe coasting**; stop **without coasting**. (pp. 13-1–13-2)
- **Trap:** Turn your wheels left while waiting to make a left turn. → **Correct:** keep the **front wheels straight ahead** until you start the turn. (p. 13-1)
- **Trap:** Pull right up to the bumper of the vehicle ahead at a light. → **Correct:** stop where you can **see its rear tires** (safe gap). (p. 13-1)
- **Trap:** Enter the intersection on a green even if traffic is backed up on the far side. → **Correct:** **don't enter** if there is **not enough room to clear** it. (p. 13-2)
- **Trap:** After a left turn onto a multi-lane road, pick any lane. → **Correct:** finish in the lane **directly right of the center line**, then move to the **right-most lane** when safe. (pp. 13-2, 13-4)
- **Trap:** In the stop/start, leave the right signal on while parked. → **Correct:** **cancel** the signal and turn on the **4-way flashers**, set the parking brake, go to Neutral/Park, feet off the pedals. (p. 13-3)
- **Trap:** Turn the wheel before you roll so you pull out faster. → **Correct:** **don't turn the wheel before the vehicle moves**; pull **straight ahead** first. (p. 13-3)
- **Trap:** Brake in the middle of the curve if it feels fast. → **Correct:** slow down **before** the curve so no braking or shifting is needed **in** it. (p. 13-3)
- **Trap:** Downshift while crossing the tracks for more power. → **Correct:** **don't stop, change gears, pass or change lanes** while any part of the vehicle is in the crossing. (p. 13-3)
- **Trap:** A placarded truck should stop as close to the tracks as possible to see well. → **Correct:** stop **no less than 15 feet and no more than 50 feet** from the **nearest rail**. (p. 13-3)
- **Trap:** Shift gears in the middle of a turn to keep power. → **Correct:** **don't shift in turns or intersections**; change gears **before** the turn. (pp. 13-1, 13-4)
- **Trap:** Pumping the brakes is the smooth way to stop. → **Correct:** don't **ride or pump** the brake; brake **smoothly with steady pressure**. (p. 13-4)
- **Trap:** If the route has no railroad crossing, that part is skipped. → **Correct:** you may be asked to **explain and demonstrate** the procedure at a **simulated** location. (p. 13-3)

## One-minute recap
- Pass = **no more than 30 errors** and **no critical errors** (a critical error = **automatic failure**). English only; **2** warnings, **3rd** = fail; **3 attempts** in total.
- Always: **safety belt**, obey every law, **no crash or moving violation**. Missing situations may be **simulated**.
- Turns: signal, slow smoothly, **no coasting** (out of gear longer than your vehicle), stop where you see the **rear tires** ahead, **wheels straight** while waiting, watch the **inside** of the turn.
- Intersections: don't enter without room to clear; **no lane changes** in them; yield to pedestrians.
- Lane changes **left then right**; expressway: merge smoothly, slow down **in the exit lane**.
- Stop/start: **right** signal in, then cancel it, **flashers on**, parking brake, Neutral/Park, feet off; out: flashers off, **left** signal, pull **straight** before steering.
- Curves: slow **before**. Rail: never stop, shift, pass or change lanes on the tracks; buses/school buses/placards stop **15–50 feet** from the nearest rail, flashers on then **off after crossing**.
- Always scored: clutch (double-clutch, no riding/popping), gears (no grinding, **no shifting in turns**), brakes (**smooth, steady**), lanes (left turn ends **right of the center line**, right turn in the **curb lane**), **both hands** on the wheel, mirrors, signals.
