# SK-02 · The basic control skills test

> **Handbook:** Section 12, pages 12-1–12-4 · **Test:** Skills Tests · **Exam weight:** Medium · **Study time:** ~30 min

## What you'll be able to answer
- What the basic control skills test checks, and which **6 exercises** you may be given.
- The **4 things** the examiner scores: encroachments, pull-ups, looks, and final position.
- What counts as a **pull-up** and a **look**, and how many looks you get (**2**, but only **1** on straight line backing).
- How to stop and get out safely for a look — and which mistakes can be an **automatic failure**.
- What "finished" means for each exercise: straight line, offset backs, both parallel parks, and the alley dock (**within 3 feet** of the back).

## Learn it

The basic control skills test is the **second** skills test. It happens on a practice area marked with **lines and cones**, not on the street. The examiner wants to see that you can **control the vehicle** at low speed and **judge where it is** compared with lines and objects around it. These same skills help you in real driving, like backing into a loading dock. (p. 12-1)

You must pass the vehicle inspection test before you take this one. (p. 11-11)

**Key words**
- **Exercise** = one of the set maneuvers on the test course, such as parallel parking.
- **Boundary** = the edge of the exercise area, marked by painted lines or cones.
- **Encroachment** = touching or crossing a boundary line or cone with any part of your vehicle.
- **Pull-up** = stopping and then driving forward to fix a position or clear an encroachment.
- **Look** = getting out of the seat (or opening the door, or walking back on a bus) to see where your vehicle is.
- **Final position** = where and how the examiner told you the vehicle must end up.
- **Offset back** = backing out of one lane and into the lane next to it.
- **Alley dock** = backing into a narrow space at a right angle, like backing up to a loading dock.
- **Sight-side** = the side you can see from the driver's seat, which is the **driver's (left) side**. Backing toward it lets you watch the rear out your side window (p. 2-10).
- **3 points of contact** = keeping three of your hands and feet on the vehicle at all times while you climb in or out.

### 12 Test rules (p. 12-1)
- **No interpreters.** The whole test is done **in English**. (p. 12-1)
- The language warning rule is the same as for all skills tests: **2 verbal warnings**, and the **3rd** offense on the same test date is an **automatic failure**. (p. 12-1)
- **Backup cameras and self-parking** technology **cannot be the only thing** you use to do the test. *Why:* the test is about **your** skill in judging the vehicle's position. (p. 12-1)
- You will be tested on **some** (a subset) of these **6** exercises: straight line backing, offset back/right, offset back/left, parallel park (driver side), parallel park (conventional), and alley dock. (p. 12-1)
- **Refusing** an exercise, or **not finishing it** as told, **may be an automatic failure**. (p. 12-1)
- You have **3 attempts in total** to pass the vehicle inspection, basic control skills and road tests. (p. 11-1)

### 12.1 Scoring: encroachments and pull-ups (p. 12-1)
The examiner scores **4 things**:
1. **Encroachments** (crossing boundaries)
2. **Pull-ups**
3. **Outside vehicle observations (looks)**
4. **Final position** / inside parallel

**Encroachments.** Each time any part of your vehicle **touches or crosses** a boundary line or cone, it counts as **one error**. (p. 12-1)

**Pull-ups.** When you **stop and pull forward** to clear an encroachment or get a better position, that is a pull-up. (p. 12-1)
- Just **stopping** — without changing direction — is **not** a pull-up.
- Your **first (initial) pull-ups are not penalized**, but **too many** pull-ups count as errors.
- *Why:* real drivers pull up to fix a bad angle; the test allows some, but it rewards getting it right with few corrections.
- *Worked example:* you back up, stop to think, then keep backing. That stop is **not** a pull-up (you did not change direction). If you then shift into a forward gear and drive ahead to straighten out, **that** is a pull-up.

### 12.1 Scoring: looks and getting out safely (p. 12-1)
You may be allowed to **stop safely and get out** to check where your vehicle is. This is a **look**.
- **Before you get out:** put the vehicle in **Neutral** and **set the parking brake(s)**.
- **Getting out:** **face the vehicle** and keep **3 points of contact** the whole time. On a **bus**, keep a **firm hold on the handrail** the whole time.
- If you do not **secure the vehicle** or **get out safely**, it **may be an automatic failure** of the test. *Why:* a vehicle that is not secured can roll while you are out of the seat, and climbing down carelessly can hurt you.
- **How many looks:** at most **2** per exercise — except **straight line backing**, which allows only **1**.
- **What counts as a look:** each time you **open the door**, **move out of your seat** (where you control the vehicle), or on a bus **walk to the back** to see better. (p. 12-1)

### 12.1 Scoring: final position (p. 12-1)
- Finish **every** exercise **exactly** as the examiner told you.
- If the vehicle does not end up in the **final position** described, you are **penalized** and **could fail** the test. (p. 12-1)
- *Why:* in real life, "almost in the dock" or "half in the space" is not parked.

### 12.2.1 Straight line backing (pp. 12-2–12-3)
- Back the vehicle in a **straight line** between **2 rows of cones** without touching or crossing the boundaries. (p. 12-2)
- Figure 12.1 shows a tractor-trailer sitting between two long rows of cones, with an arrow pointing straight back down the lane. (p. 12-3)
- Only **1 look** is allowed on this exercise. (p. 12-1)

### 12.2.2–12.2.3 Offset back right and left (pp. 12-2–12-3)
- **Offset back/right:** the space is to your **right rear**. **Offset back/left:** the space is to your **left rear**. (p. 12-2)
- How it goes: you drive **straight forward toward the outer boundary**. From there, you **back into the other lane** until the **front of your vehicle has passed the first set of cones** — without hitting any lines or cones. (p. 12-2)
- Figures 12.2 and 12.3 show two side-by-side lanes of cones. The vehicle starts in one lane, pulls straight ahead toward a dashed outer line, then backs on a curve into the neighboring lane. (p. 12-3)
- Tip from the backing rules: back **slowly** and, when you can, turn toward the **driver's side** so you can see; backing toward the right side is harder because you cannot see as well. (p. 2-10)

### 12.2.4–12.2.5 Parallel parking (pp. 12-2, 12-4)
| | Driver side | Conventional |
|---|---|---|
| Where the space is | On your **left** | On your **right** |
| How | Drive **past** the space's entrance, parallel to it, then **back in** | Same |
| Must not cross | Front, side or rear boundaries (cones) | Same |
| Finished when | Your **entire vehicle** is **completely inside** the space | Same |
(p. 12-2)
- Figures 12.4 and 12.5 show a tractor-trailer fully inside a box of cones next to a row of other parking spaces — one on the left side, one on the right. (p. 12-4)

### 12.2.6 Alley dock (pp. 12-2, 12-4)
- You **sight-side back** into an "alley" (a lane at a right angle). (p. 12-2)
- Drive **past** the alley and set your vehicle **parallel to the outer boundary**. Then back into the alley. (p. 12-2)
- Finish with the **rear of the vehicle within 3 feet** of the **back of the alley**, **straight** in the alley, without touching lines or cones. (p. 12-2)
- Figure 12.6 shows the vehicle's path curving back from beside the boundary line into the alley, ending straight between the cones. (p. 12-4)
- *Worked example:* you stop with the rear 5 feet from the back line. That is **more than 3 feet** — not finished. You stop 2 feet away but the trailer is angled — also not finished, because it must be **straight**.

### 2.2.4 Backing skills you will use (p. 2-10)
These general backing rules from Section 2 are what the exercises are testing:
- Start in the **proper position**; **look at your path** before you start; check the **mirrors on both sides** often. (p. 2-10)
- **Back slowly**, in the **lowest reverse gear**, so you can fix steering mistakes and stop quickly. (p. 2-10)
- **With a trailer**, turn the steering wheel the **opposite** way from where you want the trailer to go; once it starts turning, turn the wheel back to follow it. **Correct drift right away** by turning toward the drift. (p. 2-10)
- **Pull forward** (a pull-up) to reposition when needed. (p. 2-10)

## Exam traps
- **Trap:** Any time you stop while backing, it counts as a pull-up. → **Correct:** a pull-up is stopping **and pulling forward**; stopping **without changing direction** is not a pull-up. (p. 12-1)
- **Trap:** Every pull-up costs points. → **Correct:** **initial** pull-ups are **not penalized**; only an **excessive** number count as errors. (p. 12-1)
- **Trap:** You get 2 looks on every exercise. → **Correct:** **2** is the maximum, but **straight line backing** allows only **1**. (p. 12-1)
- **Trap:** Just opening the door for a quick glance is not a look. → **Correct:** **opening the door**, leaving your seat, or walking to the back of a bus **each count** as a look. (p. 12-1)
- **Trap:** Hop out quickly to look, leaving the truck in gear with your foot off the brake. → **Correct:** put it in **Neutral**, **set the parking brake(s)**, and climb down **facing the vehicle** with **3 points of contact**; not doing so may be an **automatic failure**. (p. 12-1)
- **Trap:** Brushing a cone with the mirror does not count. → **Correct:** touching or crossing a line or cone with **any part** of the vehicle is an encroachment, and **each** one is an error. (p. 12-1)
- **Trap:** You can rely on the backup camera for the exercises. → **Correct:** backup cameras and self-parking **cannot be used alone** on a skills test. (p. 12-1)
- **Trap:** Parallel park is done when most of the vehicle is in the space. → **Correct:** the **entire vehicle** must be **completely** inside the space. (p. 12-2)
- **Trap:** In the alley dock, getting the rear anywhere near the back is fine. → **Correct:** the rear must be **within 3 feet** of the back of the alley **and** the vehicle must be **straight**. (p. 12-2)
- **Trap:** "Conventional" parallel parking means the space is on your left. → **Correct:** **conventional = right** side; **driver side = left**. (p. 12-2)
- **Trap:** You may skip an exercise you find too hard and still pass. → **Correct:** **refusing** or not finishing an exercise as told **may be an automatic failure**. (p. 12-1)

## One-minute recap
- The basic control skills test is on a coned course and checks **control** and **judging position**. You get a **subset** of **6** exercises.
- **English only**; **2** warnings, **3rd** = fail. **No relying only on backup cameras.** Refusing an exercise **may fail** you.
- Scored on **4** things: **encroachments** (each touch or cross = 1 error), **pull-ups**, **looks**, **final position**.
- A **pull-up** = stop **and pull forward**. First ones are free; **too many** = errors. A plain stop is not a pull-up.
- **Looks:** max **2**, only **1** on straight line backing. Opening the door, leaving your seat, or walking back on a bus each count.
- Before a look: **Neutral + parking brake**. Climb out **facing the vehicle** with **3 points of contact** (bus: hold the handrail) — or risk automatic failure.
- Offset back: until the **front passes the first cones**. Parallel park: **driver side = left**, **conventional = right**, **whole vehicle inside**.
- Alley dock: sight-side back, rear **within 3 feet** of the back, vehicle **straight**. Back **slowly**, toward the **driver's side** when you can.
