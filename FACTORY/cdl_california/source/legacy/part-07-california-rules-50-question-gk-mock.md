# B7 — California-specific rules, codes, and a 50-question GK mock

## B7.1 California rules

**Speed and lanes**
- Trucks with 3+ axles and any vehicle towing: **55 mph** maximum even where the posted limit is higher (CVC 22406).
- Use the right lane, or either of the two rightmost lanes where there are 4+ lanes in your direction, except to pass or turn (CVC 21655).

**Alcohol** — 0.04 BAC in a CMV is DUI; under 21 it is 0.01; any detectable alcohol brings a 24-hour out-of-service order.

**Phones and headsets**
- Handheld phone use is banned for all drivers (CVC 23123.5); hands-free single-touch is allowed.
- You may not wear a headset or earplugs covering **both** ears (CVC 27400).

**Lights**
- Headlights on when wipers run continuously, or when visibility is under **1,000 ft** (CVC 24400).
- Dim high beams within **500 ft** of oncoming traffic and **300 ft** when following (CVC 24409).

**Railroad crossings** — placarded hazmat vehicles, buses, and tank vehicles stop **15–50 ft** from the nearest rail (CVC 22452). Never shift gears while crossing.

**Idling** — diesel vehicles over 10,000 lb GVWR may not idle more than **5 minutes** at any location (13 CCR 2485); sleeper-berth idling is also restricted.

**Weigh stations** — commercial vehicles must stop when a station is open or when directed by an officer.

**Chain controls** — trucks and vehicles towing must chain up whenever R1–R3 controls are in effect, per the posted signs. Carry chains in chain-control areas.

**Crash reporting** — file an SR-1 with the DMV within **10 days** if there was injury, death, or over $1,000 in damage.

**Size limits (standard; permits and the STAA network differ)**

| Dimension | Limit | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Width | 102 in. | CVC 35100 |
| Height | 14 ft | CVC 35250 |
| Single vehicle length | 40 ft | CVC 35400 |
| Combination length | 65 ft | CVC 35401 |
| KPRA, 2+ axle semitrailer | 40 ft | CVC 35401.5 |
| KPRA, single-axle semitrailer | 38 ft | CVC 35401.5 |

**Intrastate hours of service** — 12 hours driving, 16-hour window, 80 hours in 8 consecutive days.

**Medical certificate** — MCSA-5876 valid up to 2 years, filed with the DMV; a lapse downgrades the CDL.

**Employer Pull Notice (EPN)** — CA employers enroll to receive their drivers' DMV records automatically.

**Ages** — intrastate from 18 (with an intrastate-only restriction under 21); interstate and hazmat at 21.

**Triples** are not legal on California roads, though the handbook still teaches them.
