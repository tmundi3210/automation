# B11 — P endorsement: Transporting Passengers (20 questions, need 16)

Required for a vehicle designed to carry **16 or more people including the driver**.

## B11.1 Vehicle inspection

Standard 7-step inspection, plus:
- Every seat safely secured to the bus.
- Passenger signaling devices, handholds and railings, and lighting all working.
- **Emergency exits** and roof hatches: not damaged, closing securely, with working warning devices; emergency exit handles must not be tampered with.
- No loose baggage blocking the aisle, the door, or an exit.
- Service and emergency doors, mirrors, and the restroom (if fitted) in order.
- Never drive with an open emergency exit or an open roof hatch.
- A bus may **not** have recapped, regrooved, or retreaded tires on the front wheels.

## B11.2 Hazmat a bus may not carry

**Forbidden in a bus carrying people:**
- Division 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 explosives.
- Division 2.3 poison gas.
- Liquid Class 6 poison, tear gas, irritating material.
- Any amount of radioactive material **in the space occupied by people**.
- Any hazmat labeled POISON or POISON GAS, or placarded (other than the limited exceptions below).

**Allowed, with limits:**
- Small-arms ammunition (ORM-D), emergency shipments of drugs, chemicals, and hospital supplies.
- Up to **100 lb of solid Class 6 poisons**.
- Total allowed hazmat, other than those exceptions, must not exceed **500 lb**, and no more than **100 lb of any one class**.

Riders are never allowed on a bus carrying prohibited hazmat.

## B11.3 Loading and passenger management

- Do not allow riders near the driver's seat or in front of the **standee line** (or a 2-ft line behind the driver's seat). All standing riders must stay **behind** it.
- Baggage and freight must be secured so it doesn't fall on riders, block the aisle or door, or prevent a safe exit.
- Announce stops, and give riders time to get seated before starting.
- Watch riders' behavior; don't discharge a disruptive rider where it would be unsafe — wait for a scheduled stop or a well-lit place where they can get help.
- After the trip, check for sleeping riders, articles left behind, and damage.
- Charter buses: don't allow passengers aboard until departure time.

## B11.4 On the road

**Railroad crossings:** stop **15–50 ft** from the nearest rail, look and listen in both directions, and never shift gears while crossing. **Exempt** (no stop required): streetcar crossings, industrial switching tracks, crossings controlled by a police officer or flagman, a green traffic light, and tracks marked "exempt" or "abandoned."

**Drawbridges:** stop at least **50 ft** before the draw, look both ways, then cross when it is safe. **No stop required** when there is a traffic light showing green, an attendant or signal controlling traffic, or a signal light.

**At stops:** apply the parking brake whenever you leave the seat.

**Fueling:** never refuel in a closed building with riders aboard; avoid fueling with riders aboard if possible.

**Other rules:** don't talk with riders or engage in distracting activity while driving; watch for the bus's greater height and tail swing; common bus crashes happen at intersections, so use caution even with a green light; use caution with the standee line, the interior mirror, and passenger behavior.

**Prohibited practices:** no towing or pushing a disabled bus with riders aboard unless getting off would be unsafe, and then only to the nearest safe spot.

# B12 — S endorsement: School Buses (20 questions, need 16)

Requires the **P** endorsement as well. In California, school bus drivers also need a Special Driver Certificate, a DOJ/FBI background check, and first-aid training.

## B12.1 Danger zones and mirrors

**Danger zone:** the area on all sides of the bus where students are at greatest risk.
- **Front:** up to **30 ft** from the front bumper, with the first 10 ft the most dangerous.
- **Left and right sides:** **10 ft**.
- **Rear:** **10 ft** behind the rear bumper.
- The left side is always dangerous because of passing traffic.

**Mirror adjustment**

| Mirror | Must show |
|---|---|
| Flat (side) mirrors | **200 ft** (about 4 bus lengths) behind, along both sides, including the rear tires touching the ground |
| Convex mirrors | The entire side of the bus up to the mirror mounts, the front of the rear tires touching the ground, and at least one traffic lane each side |
| Crossover mirrors | The entire front bumper area to the point of direct vision, both front tires touching the ground, and the area from the front to the service door |
| Overhead inside mirror | The top of the rear window and every student's head |

Objects in convex and crossover mirrors look smaller and distorted; use them together with the flat mirrors.

## B12.2 Loading and unloading

Loading and unloading is the **most dangerous** part of the route; most student fatalities happen in the danger zone outside the bus.

- Activate the alternating flashing amber lights about **200 ft** (5–10 seconds) before the stop.
- Stop at least **10 ft** from waiting students, so they must walk to the bus and you can see them.
- Activate the red flashing lights and extend the stop arm; check all mirrors.
- Students crossing walk about **10 ft** along the side and cross at least **10 ft in front** of the right corner of the bumper, so you can see them and they can see you.
- Count the students on and off; know where every student is before moving.
- After a stop, check all mirrors again, especially for a student returning for a dropped item, before releasing the brakes.
- **Tail swing** can reach **up to 3 ft**, so check clearance when pulling away.

**Backing:** strongly discouraged. Never back with students outside the bus. Post a lookout or get out and check. Make sure all students are aboard before backing.

## B12.3 Railroad crossings

- Stop **no closer than 15 ft and no farther than 50 ft** from the nearest rail.
- **Open the service door and the driver's window**, turn off radios and noisy equipment, and silence the passengers.
- Look and listen in both directions.
- Cross in a **low gear** and **never shift gears while crossing**.
- Do not proceed unless you can clear the tracks completely.
- California uses flashing red lights at rail crossings for school buses carrying students.

## B12.4 Emergency exit and evacuation

**Evacuate when:** the bus is on fire or a fire is near; the bus is stalled on or next to a railroad crossing; the bus's position may change and increase danger; there is an imminent risk of collision; or there is a hazardous materials spill.

- Move students at least **100 ft off the road**, in the direction of oncoming traffic, and keep them together.
- Know the mandatory evacuation drill and the location of every emergency exit.

## B12.5 Other rules

- **Post-trip:** walk through the whole bus, checking for sleeping students, items left behind, damage, and mechanical problems.
- **Student management:** handle discipline at the next stop, never while driving; assign seats if needed.
- **Antilock brakes, strobe light, and emergency equipment** must be checked in the pre-trip.
- Watch the bus's high center of gravity on curves and ramps.
