# B1 — Licensing, disqualifications, vehicle inspection (GK)

## B1.1 Classes and CLP

- **Class A:** a combination with GCWR (combined weight rating) ≥26,001 lb, where the towed unit's GVWR is >10,000 lb. A Class A holder can also drive Class B and C vehicles with the right endorsements.
- **Class B:** a single vehicle ≥26,001 lb, or one towing ≤10,000 lb.
- **Class C:** under Class B weights but either carries 16+ people including the driver, or carries placarded hazmat.

**Age:** 18 for intrastate driving. 21 for interstate or hazmat.

**CLP rules:**
- Wait 14 days after getting the CLP before the skills test.
- Be accompanied by a CDL holder with the proper class and endorsements, seated beside you.
- A CLP holder may not carry passengers (P/S), drive a tanker unless it is empty and purged, or carry hazmat (H).
- ELDT must be completed before the Class A skills test.

**Other license rules:**
- You may hold only one license, from one state.
- A DOT medical card (Medical Examiner's Certificate, MCSA-5876) is valid for up to 2 years and must be kept current with the DMV. If it lapses, the CDL is downgraded.

**Notification duties:**
- Tell your employer within **30 days** of any traffic conviction, in any vehicle.
- Tell your home state within **30 days** of an out-of-state conviction.
- Tell your employer **by the end of the next business day** if your license is suspended, revoked, or cancelled, or if you are disqualified.
- When applying, give your employer **10 years** of employment history.

## B1.2 Alcohol, implied consent, disqualification

**BAC limits:**
- In a CMV: **0.04** is DUI.
- Under 21 in CA: **0.01**.
- Any detectable alcohol, or alcohol within 4 hours of duty: **24-hour out-of-service order**.
- Implied consent: by driving a CMV you agree to testing. Refusing counts the same as a DUI.

**Major offenses:**
- The list: DUI; refusing a test; leaving the scene of a crash; a felony using a CMV; driving a CMV with a revoked or suspended CDL; causing a fatality through negligent CMV operation.
- 1st offense: **1-year** disqualification, or **3 years** if hauling hazmat.
- 2nd offense: **lifetime** (10-year reinstatement possible in some cases).
- Using a CMV in a controlled-substance felony (manufacture/distribution), or in human trafficking: **lifetime, no reinstatement**.

**Serious traffic violations** — 2 within 3 years: **60 days**. 3 within 3 years: **120 days**.
- Speeding 15+ mph over the limit; reckless driving; improper or erratic lane change; following too closely; a violation contributing to a fatal crash; driving a CMV without a CDL, without it in your possession, or without the proper class or endorsement; texting; using a handheld phone.

**Railroad-highway grade crossing violations:**
- 1st: ≥60 days. 2nd within 3 years: ≥120 days. 3rd+ within 3 years: ≥1 year.
- Violations: not slowing and checking the tracks; not stopping when required; stopping without enough clearance; not obeying a signal or officer; not having room to clear the tracks; getting stuck from insufficient undercarriage clearance.

**Out-of-service (OOS) order violations:**
- 1st: 180 days–1 year. 2nd within 10 years: 2–5 years. 3rd+: 3–5 years.
- Carrying hazmat or 16+ passengers: 1st is 180 days–2 years; 2nd and 3rd are 3–5 years.

**Phones:** texting or handheld use is a serious violation. Fines up to **$2,750** for the driver and **$11,000** for the employer. Hands-free, single-touch use is allowed.

## B1.3 Inspection

**Types:**
- **Pre-trip:** required before every trip.
- **En route:** check cargo and securement within the first **50 miles**, then every **150 miles or 3 hours**, whichever comes first, and after every break.
- **Post-trip:** complete a DVIR. The motor carrier fixes and certifies defects; the next driver reviews and signs.
- Police or DOT may place a vehicle out of service for unsafe defects.

**Key thresholds:**
- Tire tread: **≥4/32"** in every major groove on steer tires; **≥2/32"** on all others.
- No regrooved, recapped, or retreaded tires on the front wheels of a bus.
- No mismatched tire sizes, radial mixed with bias-ply, or dual tires touching each other or the body.
- Steering play: no more than **10°**, about 2" at the rim of a 20" wheel.
- Loose lug nuts: rust streaks around the nuts. Cracked or welded rims are defects.
- Brake drums and shoes: no cracks. Linings must not be thin, loose, or contaminated with oil or grease.
- Slack adjusters: more than about **1"** of travel when pulled by hand means adjustment is needed.
- Suspension: cracked or broken spring hangers, missing or broken leaves, or shifted leaves that could hit a tire. A missing leaf in a multi-leaf spring is dangerous. Check shocks for leaks and torque rods for damage.
- Exhaust: leaks, loose parts, parts rubbing fuel or brake lines, carbon soot at joints.
- Frame: cracks, bends, missing cross members, cracked or missing floor.
- **Emergency equipment:** a properly rated and charged fire extinguisher; spare electrical fuses (unless the vehicle has circuit breakers); **3 red reflective warning triangles**. Optional: chains, tire tools, emergency phone numbers, accident kit.

**Seven-step method:**
1. **Vehicle overview.** Look for leaks or damage; review the last DVIR.
2. **Engine compartment** (engine off, parking brake set): oil, coolant, power steering fluid; hoses for leaks or cracks; belts for tightness (about ½–¾" of play at the center), cracks, fraying; air compressor belt if belt-driven.
3. **Start the engine, check inside the cab:** oil pressure rises to normal within seconds; air pressure builds 50→90 psi within 3 minutes; ammeter/voltmeter normal; coolant temperature rising gradually; warning lights off; mirrors, windshield, wipers.
4. **Lights:** turn off the engine, confirm the parking brake holds, turn on headlights (low and high) and 4-way flashers.
5. **Walk-around:** lights and reflectors (red at the rear, amber elsewhere); tires, wheels, suspension, brakes, fuel tank cap; coupling (see B6); cargo securement and doors.
6. **Signal lights:** turn signals and brake lights.
7. **Start the engine and test the brakes:**
   - Parking brake: pull gently in low gear; the vehicle should not move.
   - Service brake: at about 5 mph, brake firmly. No pulling to one side, no spongy pedal.
   - Hydraulic brakes: pump 3 times, hold 5 seconds; the pedal should not move.
   - Air brakes: see B5.

**Other inspection points:**
- A leaning vehicle may mean a flat tire, shifted load, or broken suspension.
- Batteries secured, no leaks, vent cap in place.
- Horn and heater/defroster must work. A seat belt is required.
- Check tire inflation with a gauge; kicking a tire is not reliable.
- Hazmat: check tires at every stop; check placards.
