## 3. THE BUILD / REVERSE-ENGINEER / AI VERDICT — reconciled and made blunt

### 3a. Build-your-own: **Do not pursue.** (highest-confidence finding in T3)
A computerised V-bed flat machine's *defining* subsystems — **needle bed, needles, cam systems, electronic needle-selection, controller firmware, knit-CAD** — are **all VERY HARD** (B §1 teardown) and are exactly the parts you cannot skip or fake. **You can build the easy 20% (frame, take-down, guards) and still not have a knitting machine.** The precision wall is real: gauge pitch/flatness to a fraction of ~2–3.6 mm over a 1–1.7 m bed, repeatable after thermal cycling and millions of strokes (B §2). **Even Shima and Stoll buy their needles from Groz-Beckert** [FACT — groz-beckert.com] — a micro-brand machining its own needles is choosing to lose to a 170-year metallurgy specialist at its own game. The open-source tell: OpenKnit proved a hobby machine is possible but nowhere near commercial gauge/speed/reliability/FF-shaping; and the serious academic effort (CMU Textiles Lab, **knitout** format, knit compilers, 2023 ACM TOG) is **all on the software layer, not the iron** [FACT]. **Build cost ≈ [ESTIMATE] $2M–10M+ over 3–7 yr for a likely-inferior result vs a ~$15k used Chinese unit** — disproportionate to a brand whose edge is product + AI design + export-legitimacy.

### 3b. Reverse-engineering / own-parts / 3D-printing: **periphery + maintenance ONLY.**
- **Do:** jigs, fixtures, alignment gauges, non-critical spares (yarn-guide eyelets, carrier brackets, guards, cone holders, tensioner add-ons), take-down/auxiliary-tensioning tweaks, **reverse-engineering for *maintenance*** (learn cam timing/sensors to keep cheap used iron running), wear-part 3D-scanning to replace obsolete brackets. This saves real money on uptime. ✅
- **Don't:** needles, needle beds, cam systems, electronic needle-selection, controller firmware/knit-CAD cloning. The core kinematic chain (bed → needles → cams → selection) destroys money and time, and cloning selection/firmware adds **IP/legal exposure**. ❌
- **Rule:** periphery yes, **core kinematic chain never.** `[ESTIMATE: engineering judgment, HIGH]`

### 3c. AI: real leverage on software/data/vision, **hype on the iron.**
- **Real (do this):** (1) **generative design → knit program** (T8 bridge; vendor stacks already do design→simulation→virtual-sampling [FACT]; AI assist drafts stitch structures/shaping/auto-grading on top — the single highest-value AI use in T3, because it offsets the scarce-programmer bottleneck); (2) **CAD-programming assist** as a *programmer multiplier, not replacement*; (3) **predictive maintenance** on used iron (high value *because* we'd run cheap used machines); (4) **vision defect detection** (T5 backbone). ✅
- **Hype / overstated:** "AI designs and builds the machine" — **No**, AI doesn't solve metrology/cam-profile/needle-selection (physics + manufacturing, not generation) ❌; "generative AI → finished garment, no programmer" — overstated, AI drafts and a human + real sampling close the loop ⚠️; "AI replaces the linker/QC entirely" — vision QC *assists*, doesn't yet replace the skilled hand seam ⚠️.

## 4. THE STOLL EXIT — the one hard market-structure [FACT] both must carry

**[FACT — multiple independent sources, Oct 2025: karlmayer.com, knittingindustry.com, Textile World, wtin.com]:** KARL MAYER (which acquired Stoll in 2020) decided in early 2025 to refocus on warp knitting / technical textiles and **discontinue the STOLL flat-knitting machine business**. The **Reutlingen (Germany) production site closed 31 Oct 2025**; **China production ended Dec 2025**; ~280 Reutlingen jobs lost; no investor found; **spare-parts/service continue via the Obertshausen central warehouse.**

**Reconciled implication (A §2 raised it; both endorse the read):**
- **New Stoll flat machines are effectively end-of-line.** New-build choices narrow to **Shima Seiki (premium/standard, India support) + Chinese brands led by Cixing (cost tier; Cixing also owns Swiss Steiger).**
- **The installed base becomes a used/service market** → the used pool *deepens* (lots of ex-Stoll iron will circulate).
- **For ex-Stoll used purchases, service availability is now a real diligence item** — parts via Obertshausen only; factor it into any used-Stoll buy. `[FACT on closure; ESTIMATE on cost impact]`
- **Net for us:** this *reinforces* buy-used-or-subcontract and slightly raises the relative attractiveness of Shima (for new/support) and Chinese used (for cost). `[ESTIMATE: judgment, MED-HIGH]`

India remains **an importer/assembler/dealer market, not a builder** of computerised flat / WHOLEGARMENT machines (Ludhiana dealers e.g. Kniton Traders, King International handle Shima/Stoll/Cixing + parts/repair). `[FACT dealer existence; ESTIMATE "primarily importer," HIGH — no major Indian computerised-flat OEM found]`

### 5b. Throughput — order-of-magnitude planning bands only [ESTIMATE, LOW–MED]

## 7. Reconciled bottom line (for the DEEPDIVE writer)

1. **Subcontract, don't own, don't build.** At capsule scale, knit+link+finish via the Ludhiana job-work mesh; own only design + brand + (optionally) one cheap sampling hand-flat. Buy **used** iron — **Chinese (Cixing) first** — only when a repeating program hits proven volume. **New is rarely the move, and Stoll new-build is off the table (2025 exit) → Shima or Chinese for new.** Whole-garment (no-linking) is a later/volume trade study, not a launch choice.
2. **The constraint is the programmer and the linker, not the machine.** Rent/contract the scarce **CAD/flat-knit programmer** (Shima SDS-ONE APEX / Stoll M1plus); don't own the linking bottleneck early. Local programmer availability = T1 Open Q#8, still to confirm on the ground.
3. **Building your own machine: don't — highest-confidence verdict in T3.** Needle bed/needles/cams/selection/firmware/knit-CAD are all very-hard, decades-deep, supplier-defended (Groz-Beckert needles; Shima/Stoll CAD). ~$2M–10M+/3–7 yr for a likely-inferior result vs a sub-$20k used machine. Reverse-engineer/3D-print **only the periphery + for maintenance**, never the core kinematic chain.
4. **AI's real payoff is software/data/vision** — design→program assist (offsets the scarce programmer; T8), predictive maintenance on used iron, vision defect detection (T5 backbone). **"AI builds the machine" is hype.**
5. **Cost/skill picture:** used-machine price bands are **wide and LOW–MED confidence** — get landed quotes (freight + duty under HSN 8447, ~7.5% BCD + 18% IGST + install) before T4's cost model commits. The scarce, winnable asset is **the program**, not the metal.
