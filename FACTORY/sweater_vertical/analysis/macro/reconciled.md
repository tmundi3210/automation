## 2. Where they genuinely conflict — and which side the evidence favours

There are **five** real disagreements. Most are disagreements of *framing and burden-of-proof*, not of fact — which is itself the single most important reconciliation finding. I rule on each.

### C1 — "Every lever is in the owner's control except CAC" (advocate) vs. "6 of 9 load-bearing joints are uncontrolled or unmeasured" (neutralize)
- **Advocate's claim** (§1, §7.4): every winning lever is a controllable design/pricing/merchandising choice except CAC, and CAC is cheaply testable — so the bet is "disciplined, not reckless."
- **Neutralize's claim** (§1, §3): false on the advocate's own table — repeat rate (#2), forward duty (#4), organic reach (#5), anti-pill physics (#6), AI-program autonomy (#8) are all outside unilateral control; #3 (cost), #7 (working capital), #9 (scale) are merely unmeasured.
- **EVIDENCE FAVOURS: NEUTRALIZE (clearly).** The advocate's sentence is literally false against its own §1 table. Pricing, line-lead, make-vs-buy, operating model and fixed-nut *are* controllable (advocate is right there) — but **repeat rate, organic-reach conversion, post-wash pilling physics, AI→knit-program autonomy, and forward duty are not things the owner sets by choosing**; they are set by customers, physics, and policy. **DOWNGRADE the advocate's framing** from "every lever controllable except CAC" to: *"the merchandising and capital-structure levers are controllable; the demand-side, materials-physics, and policy levers are not — they are testable or estimable, which is a weaker claim."* This is the advocate's single biggest over-claim and the memo must carry the corrected version.
  - **But downgrade the neutralize too:** its phrasing implies these uncontrolled joints are *unknowable*. They are not — **CAC, anti-pill grade, and AI-program autonomy are cheaply** ***testable*** (one ad test, one PPS swatch, one PPS program), and forward duty is **re-confirmable per PO + stress-tested at 25–50%**. "Uncontrolled" ≠ "un-de-riskable." The honest synthesis: **the owner cannot *control* the outcome of six joints, but he can cheaply *measure* most of them before betting — which is exactly why the test-first sequence (R9) is the resolution to this entire conflict.**

### C2 — Burden of proof: "conditional GO" (advocate) vs. "NO-GO-until-measured / GATED" (neutralize)
- **Advocate** (§9, §10): rates it a GO on the inverted config, with the tests as confirming "first moves."
- **Neutralize** (§6): rates it GATED — NO-GO today, because *not one load-bearing joint carries a [FACT]*; the tests are a **hard gate**, not a first move; inventory cash forbidden until gates pass.
- **EVIDENCE FAVOURS: NEUTRALIZE on status, ADVOCATE on action — they are reconcilable and not actually opposed.** The neutralize is right that **today's evidentiary status is GATED**: a plan with nine [UNKNOWN]/[ESTIMATE-LOW] joints and zero [FACT]s under its *viability* has not earned the word "GO." The advocate is right that **the correct *response* to a gated, bounded-downside, cheap-to-test plan is to fund the tests, not to walk away.** The only substantive difference is whether the test sequence is described as a "first move" (advocate, softer) or a "hard gate" (neutralize, firmer). **The neutralize's "hard gate" is the more disciplined and the correct word** — because the advocate's "first moves … in parallel" language risks the owner running the ad test *while already buying yarn*, which is the exact mistake the working-capital trap (C3) punishes. **RULING: status = GATED; action = test-don't-launch; the test sequence is a HARD GATE on inventory cash, not a parallel first step.** This is the reconciled verdict (§4).

### C3 — "Low-capital, hard-to-ruin" (advocate) vs. "the working-capital trap is the modal death and it's UNSIZED" (neutralize)
- **Advocate** (§6): low capital, high optionality, every rung reversible, downside bounded by buy-nothing.
- **Neutralize** (§2.5, §1 #7): the venture is low-*fixed-asset* capital but **not** low-*working*-capital; dye-lot MOQ (500+ units/colour) + ocean lead time + returns settlement = a months-long cash-out that "bankrupts profitable-on-paper brands," and its *size* is **[UNSIZED]** because it depends on the [UNKNOWN] dye-MOQ and [UNKNOWN] job-work rate — "you cannot fund a cycle you cannot compute."
- **EVIDENCE FAVOURS: NEUTRALIZE (clearly), but both are partly right and the distinction is the fix.** The advocate conflates two different capital questions. **Fixed/sunk capital IS low and the downside IS bounded** (no plant to strand) — advocate correct, neutralize concedes this in §5.1. **Working capital is NOT low and is the modal failure mode** — neutralize correct, and the advocate's own §8 concession ("a real constraint the owner must capitalize for") *is* the kill-case it tries to wave past. **DOWNGRADE "hard to ruin"**: the venture is **hard to ruin catastrophically (bounded sunk loss) but easy to ruin ordinarily (working-capital starvation)** — the most common way it dies is not a flopped drop but running out of cash between yarn-out and customer-paid. **The fix is mechanical and both passes imply it:** the working-capital cycle becomes computable the moment gates 2 (job-work rate) and the dye-MOQ quote land, so **sizing-and-funding the cycle is itself a gate** (neutralize gate 6), not an afterthought. Memo must carry working capital as a **named, sized, funded line item**, not fold it into "low capital."

### C4 — "Monte Carlo proves downstream margin capture works" (advocate) vs. "wrong analogy" (neutralize)
- **Advocate** (§2, §7.3): the cluster's own winners (Monte Carlo: FY26 ₹1,275.9 cr, ~17.8% EBITDA, 460+ EBOs) prove downstream-margin-capture is the right strategy; the owner runs "the same logic, redirected to US D2C."
- **Neutralize** (§3, §4): Monte Carlo is a **vertically-integrated, captive-spinning, 460-EBO incumbent** — the *structural opposite* of a buy-nothing 3-SKU micro-capsule; it proves *integration + scale + domestic retail* works, not that a US D2C capsule does.
- **EVIDENCE FAVOURS: NEUTRALIZE on the analogy, ADVOCATE on the narrow thesis it supports.** The neutralize is right that **Monte Carlo is not evidence a 3-SKU buy-nothing US capsule succeeds** — the firms are structurally unalike on every axis (integration, scale, channel, geography). **DOWNGRADE the advocate's use of Monte Carlo** from "proof the venture's strategy works" to its true, weaker form: *"a directional existence-proof that, in this cluster, the durable profit sits downstream of the loom rather than at it — which argues against competing on factory cost, and says nothing about whether THIS micro-brand's specific downstream play succeeds."* That weaker claim is still useful and still bullish-adjacent (it kills the "chase cheap yarn" temptation, R4), but it is not the load-bearing proof the advocate's §2 implies. **The neutralize slightly over-reaches too:** it is not a "wrong analogy" so much as an **over-extended** one — Monte Carlo legitimately demonstrates *direction* (downstream > loom), just not *transferability of outcome*.

### C5 — "AI-design novelty / no-pill / local authenticity is a defensible moat" (advocate) vs. "a content tactic, an unvalidated gate, and a channel — not a moat" (neutralize)
- **Advocate** (§3.3/§3.4, §9): three genuine edges — AI-design-as-organic-content engine, contractually-gated no-pill promise, "from Turlock" local authenticity — that incumbents structurally can't cheaply copy.
- **Neutralize** (§3, §2.6, §2.8): AI-design is a **near-zero-cost content tactic on a public Flux API replicable in a week** (and "AI-designed" is partly vapor until program-autonomy is proven — §2.9); no-pill is **[UNKNOWN] until PPS swatch** and one viral pilling video ends it; local authenticity is **an order-of-magnitude ~$50–110k/yr acquisition *channel*, not a market** (national TAM/SAM [UNKNOWN, refused]).
- **EVIDENCE FAVOURS: NEUTRALIZE on durability, ADVOCATE on usefulness — and the right word is "edge," not "moat."** Point by point:
  - **AI-design:** real and useful as an **organic-reach content engine at near-zero marginal cost** (advocate right that this is the cheapest weapon for an organic-acquisition model), but **not a defensible moat** — it sits on a public API and is workflow, not asset (neutralize right). **RULING: a real content/ideation EDGE, undefensible and unproven-to-convert.** Both over-state in opposite directions; "edge" is the reconciled noun.
  - **No-pill:** **promise the contractual gate (ICI ≥3–4 post-wash), never a number** — the gate is real *policy* but **unvalidated on our actual constructions until the PPS swatch**; on A2 acrylic the grade is "won at the yarn PO." **RULING: an UNVALIDATED gate; a genuine kill-risk on A2; testable cheaply (PPS swatch = a hard gate, not a claim).** Neutralize favoured.
  - **Local authenticity:** **a near-zero-CAC acquisition *channel* that caps ~$50–110k/yr [DOWNGRADED, LOW] and doubles as free R&D** (size-curve + return-rate buy-down) — the advocate's *tactical* use of it (§5) is correct and valuable, but the neutralize is right it is **a channel, not the business**; the venture only becomes more than a side project if the **unproven scale path** (national ship-to-social) clears. **RULING: keep the channel + its free byproducts; do not let it stand in for a validated market.** Neutralize favoured on scale, advocate favoured on first-revenue tactics.

  **Net C5:** the advocate's "moat" is downgraded to **"a real but undefensible content edge + an unvalidated quality gate + a low-capping acquisition channel."** The advocate's *tactical* deployment of all three (organic content engine, contractual pilling gate, Turlock seed as cheapest first revenue + free R&D) is sound and should be kept; only the word **"moat"** and the implied **durability/scale** are over-claimed.

> **The shape of every conflict:** in C1–C5 the advocate is **directionally right on the tactic and over-claims the strength/durability/controllability**; the neutralize is **right on the evidentiary status and occasionally over-reaches on un-de-riskability/analogy**. The reconciled position in every case is the advocate's *action* executed under the neutralize's *burden of proof*. There is **no conflict where the advocate's stronger claim beats the neutralize's evidence** — which is why the reconciled verdict tilts to GATED, not GO.
