# Turlock Independent-Business Catalog — first pass

*Compiled 2026-06-30 · 197 independent businesses · 74 granular categories · public-web sourced, every entry cited.*

> **This is a floor, not a ceiling.** It is a public-web first pass, deliberately limited to *independent* (non-chain) businesses, each backed by at least one source URL. Per-business **revenue is not included and is not public** — it will only ever appear later as a clearly *labeled benchmark estimate*, never as a stated fact. See the coverage caveats at the bottom for what is systematically under-counted.

**Counts.** 217 raw findings → 210 unique after dedup → **197 retained** (13 dropped as chains/unsourced/duplicate). Confidence: 138 high / 56 med / 3 low.

Systematic under-counts are significant and skew by sector: (1) home-based and by-appointment-only businesses (in-home hair/nails/lash/esthetics, bookkeepers, notaries, freelance trades) are largely invisible to the public web; (2) mobile and pop-up operations - food trucks, taco trucks, pupusa carts, mobile detailers, mobile mechanics, event florists - are badly undercounted here precisely because their only listings tend to be the generic search pages we had to drop, so real, operating trucks were almost certainly removed as unsourced; (3) non-advertising legacy businesses with no website or social presence (older trades, immigrant-owned markets, sole-proprietor professional offices) never surface; (4) anything behind bot-blocked or paywalled directories is missing - notably the Turlock Chamber of Commerce member directory and the Downtown Turlock / shopdowntownturlock.com and Visit Turlock sites, which 403 automated fetches, so the most authoritative local rosters could not be mined; (5) language and platform bias means Spanish-, Assyrian-, Punjabi-, and Portuguese-language businesses that advertise primarily by word of mouth or on community Facebook groups are underrepresented relative to their real prevalence in Turlock. Categories most likely under-counted: food trucks/taco trucks, pupuserias, panaderias/carnicerias, barbers, nail/lash/brow studios, in-home cleaners, and independent trades (handyman, painting, roofing, fencing, pool service) that barely appear here at all. Treat category_counts as lower bounds; the true independent-business population of Turlock is materially larger than 174.
