# Health & Wellness — Supply Chain & Operations (Turlock, CA)

**Scope:** Independent, owner-operated health practices in Turlock / Stanislaus County across six sub-categories — Chiropractic, Dental, Independent Gym/Fitness, Optometry, Physical Therapy, and Yoga/Wellness. Ground truth is the 26-business catalog in `businesses.json`.

**Honesty note:** Per-business revenue and margins are NOT public. Every cost %, margin, or dollar figure below is a **labeled benchmark estimate** drawn from national/industry sources and applied to the local mix — never a stated fact about a named Turlock business. Facts are tagged **[FACT]**, estimates **[ESTIMATE]**, unknowns **[UNKNOWN]**.

---

## 1. How these businesses differ structurally

Two distinct economic engines sit inside this sector:

- **Insurance-reimbursed clinical** (Dental, Optometry, Physical Therapy, and the personal-injury/auto slice of Chiropractic) — revenue gated by payer contracts (Delta Dental, VSP/EyeMed, Medicare, commercial PPOs) and, heavily in Stanislaus County, **Medi-Cal / Denti-Cal**. Operations revolve around billing, prior auth, and a physical-goods supply chain (crowns, frames, lenses, modalities). **[FACT]**
- **Cash / membership wellness** (independent gyms, yoga, barre, and the cash/maintenance slice of Chiropractic) — revenue is membership/class-pack/cash-pay, almost no payer billing, and the "supply chain" is mostly capital equipment + facility + instructor labor rather than consumables. **[FACT]**

Stanislaus County's high Medi-Cal mix, CSU Stanislaus + retiree + ag-worker demand base, and competition from Modesto hospital systems and DSO/chain optical (e.g., corporate optical, dental groups) shape which model each independent leans on. **[FACT]**

## 3. Operating model & labor

**Labor model by sub-category:**
- **Dental / Optometry / PT:** W-2 licensed pros + support staff (hygienists, opticians, PT aides, front-desk/billing). Some optometry and dental practices add associate doctors. Labor is the single largest cost line. **[FACT]**
- **Chiropractic:** Predominantly **owner-operator** DCs, often solo or with one CA (chiropractic assistant) + billing; PI/auto practices (Dr. Jones) carry heavier billing/lien admin. **[FACT]**
- **Gym / Yoga / Barre:** Mix of **owner-operator + per-class-paid instructors** (frequently 1099 independent contractors, though CA AB-5/Dynamex tests pressure misclassification). Instructor pay scales directly with class volume. **[FACT]**

**Key cost drivers:**
- **Rent vs. mobile/contract:** All catalog businesses are fixed-location (clinics/studios) — none mobile — so commercial rent is a structural fixed cost. **[FACT]** Turlock commercial rent is materially below coastal CA; benchmark CA commercial $10–$40/sq ft/yr. **[ESTIMATE]**
- **Labor %:** dominant in clinical and studio models alike (see §4).
- **COGS:** high in dental (lab + supplies) and optometry (frames/lenses/contacts); near-zero in gym/yoga/chiro-cash.
- **Insurance/licensing/bond:** malpractice/professional liability (~$1,200/mo benchmark for solo clinical), general liability, plus state license/CE fees. **[ESTIMATE/FACT]**

**Daypart & seasonality:**
- Clinical (dental/optometry/PT/chiro): weekday daytime + some early-evening/Saturday blocks; optometry sees a Q4/January bump as flex-spending/vision benefits reset and a back-to-school spike (CSU Stanislaus). **[ESTIMATE]**
- Fitness/yoga: classic **early-morning + evening dayparts**, strong **January "resolution" surge** then summer dip; membership smooths cash flow. **[ESTIMATE]**

## 4. Cost structure (labeled estimates)

> **Method:** national industry benchmarks (ADA Health Policy Institute / dental CPA surveys, optometry GPO/COGS surveys, APTA/PT practice economics, boutique-fitness and yoga operating models) applied to a low-rent Central Valley market. These are **estimates**, not figures for any named business. Confidence words noted.

**Dental** (medium confidence) — total overhead benchmark **~55–65% of collections**; sub-$750k-collection practices often **70–80%**. Component estimates: staff payroll **25–28%**, lab fees **6–8%**, supplies **5–6%**, rent/facility **6–7%**. **[ESTIMATE]** Turlock practices skew to the higher-overhead small-practice band given solo/single-doctor footprints. Denti-Cal reimbursement runs well below commercial PPO with heavier admin, compressing margin on Medi-Cal-heavy patient mix. **[FACT]**

**Optometry** (medium confidence) — optical **COGS ~27–35% of revenue** (frames/lenses/contacts + shipping); buying-group membership (Vision Source/PECAA/IDOC) and tiered "minimum list price" strategies pull COGS toward the low end. **[ESTIMATE/FACT]** Practices like **Turlock Eyecare** (independent since 1950) and **Turlock Family Vision** depend on capture rate (selling glasses in-house vs. losing scripts to chain/online optical) to defend that margin. **[ESTIMATE]**

**Physical Therapy** (medium confidence) — payroll is **~70% of fixed overhead / 40–50% of total expenses**; rent **~5–10% of revenue**; admin (billing/marketing/software) **~10–15%**; supply COGS benchmark **~15%**. **[ESTIMATE]** Revenue ~**$75–$150 per visit** (often **$110–$140** when billing 3–4 codes/session under Medicare); rule of thumb a treating PT bills **3–4x salary**; **~200–400 visits/month** to clear break-even. **[ESTIMATE]** Note: 2026 Medicare conversion factor ~$33.40 with a **net ~-1% impact** after RVU changes — a structural headwind for **Altruistic**, **Elite**, and **Tower PT**. **[FACT]**

**Chiropractic** (low–medium confidence) — low consumable COGS; cost is dominated by **owner labor + rent + professional liability + EHR ($300–$800/mo)**. **[ESTIMATE/FACT]** Cash/maintenance models (Mas Vida's bilingual wellness positioning; Crawford's "affordable" positioning) carry minimal billing overhead; **PI/auto-injury** practice (Dr. Jones) trades higher per-case value for lien/collections risk and slower cash conversion. **[ESTIMATE]**

**Independent Gym/Fitness** (low–medium confidence) — in boutique operating models, **payroll is the largest line** and **lease is #2**; lease + equipment together = **~60–70% of startup cost**. Equipment leasing (Rogue/OEM financing) converts capex to monthly opex. **[ESTIMATE/FACT]** Barre Defined, Curl Fitness, and Workout Studio by KB run class/membership revenue against a high fixed-rent + instructor-labor base, so utilization (members per square foot, class fill rate) is the margin lever. **[ESTIMATE]**

**Yoga/Wellness** (low–medium confidence) — **rent ~20–30% of revenue**, **instructor pay ~$25–$75/class** (≈30%+ of top-line), targeting **~30–50% gross margin per class** after instructor + fixed cost. Unlimited memberships price ~**$100–$200/mo**. **[ESTIMATE]** I Am Yoga's broad format menu (Vinyasa→Pilates→Qi Gong) spreads fixed studio cost across more class slots, the main lever for a single-location independent. **[ESTIMATE]**

## 5. Regulatory / licensing inputs that gate operations

All clinical providers are gated by **California Department of Consumer Affairs healing-arts boards** **[FACT]**:
- **Board of Chiropractic Examiners (BCE):** annual DC renewal, **24 CE hours/yr** (incl. 2 hrs Ethics & Law). Practice X-ray adds RHB radiologic-equipment registration. **[FACT]**
- **Dental Board of California:** DDS/RDH licensure, X-ray/CBCT registration, **Denti-Cal/Medi-Cal provider enrollment** required to bill (separate from license), OSHA/Cal-OSHA infection control, hazardous/biohazard waste handling. **[FACT]**
- **California State Board of Optometry:** exam app fee **$275**, **biennial renewal $500**; glaucoma/TPA certifications expand billable medical scope. **[FACT]**
- **Physical Therapy Board of California (PTBC):** app fee **$300**, initial license **$150**, **$200 renewal**; CA allows direct access with limits. **[FACT]**

**Cross-cutting inputs:** city of Turlock business license; professional liability/malpractice insurance (lender- and board-relevant); general liability for studios (slip/fall, equipment); ADA facility compliance; for fitness/yoga, **AB-5 (Dynamex/ABC test)** worker-classification exposure on 1099 instructors. **[FACT]** **CSLB** is generally **not** a gating input for operating these practices — it governs the contractors doing the build-out, not the practice itself. **[FACT]**

## 6. Key takeaways

1. **Supply chain concentration is the dental/optometry story.** The dental "big three" (Schein/Patterson/Benco) and EssilorLuxottica/ABB give national distributors strong pricing power over small Turlock independents; **buying groups** (Vision Source/PECAA/IDOC for optical; GPOs for dental) are the main counterweight. **[FACT]**
2. **Reimbursement is the binding constraint, not supply.** In a high-Medi-Cal county, Denti-Cal rates and 2026 Medicare PT cuts pressure margins more than input costs do. **[FACT]**
3. **Cash/membership sub-categories (chiro-cash, gym, yoga) are capex + labor + rent businesses** with near-zero COGS — utilization and instructor-labor model (W-2 vs. 1099 under AB-5) are the levers. **[ESTIMATE/FACT]**
4. **Capex tiers (high→low):** dental ≈ optometry > PT > chiropractic ≈ gym > yoga. **[ESTIMATE]**
