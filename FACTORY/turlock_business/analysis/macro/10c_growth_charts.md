# 10c — Growth Charts: Turlock vs California vs Top-Growing US Cities

**What this is.** The long-run "growth chart" for Turlock and Stanislaus County (= Modesto MSA) across population, jobs, business counts, and income (~2010–2026), placed on the national growth spectrum against the fastest-growing US metros/cities and against California.

**The one-line answer:** Turlock is a **slow-growth-to-flat** market. Population grew through 2020 and has been essentially flat since; jobs recovered hard off a brutal 2010 trough but now grow under 0.5%/yr; real household income rose only ~11–12% over 13 years. Nationally, Turlock is **not** a Sunbelt-style growth market — it sits **below the US rate, an order of magnitude below Sunbelt leaders, and at-or-just-below California's faster Central Valley peers.** The strategic inheritance: **no demographic tailwind — growth must come from share-of-wallet and regional capture, not new arrivals.**

> **HONESTY RULE (applies throughout).** Every government / census / tax / budget / economic figure is either a **CITED PUBLIC FACT** (with source URL), a **clearly LABELED ESTIMATE** (method + confidence), or marked **UNKNOWN**. No private business's revenue is stated as fact anywhere. No numbers are fabricated; figures that could not be sourced are flagged.
>
> **Data-access caveat.** On 2026-06-30, direct automated fetches of several primary portals returned HTTP 403 — `census.gov` / `data.census.gov`, `bls.gov`, `fred.stlouisfed.org`, `dof.ca.gov`, `cityofturlock.org`. Figures therefore come from **search-indexed summaries** of those same official datasets and reputable aggregators (Wikipedia citing decennial Census; Census news-release summaries; Neilsberg / Point2Homes / Data USA republishing ACS/PEP; Turlock Journal citing CA DOF). Each figure carries its source. A direct-access follow-up pass (data.census.gov ACS/CBP, FRED, EDD, CA DOF E-2) would pin several series to exact published values; the open items are listed in §9.

## 8. Strategic Conclusion

1. **No demographic tailwind.** Turlock is **flat**, not growing. Growth must come from **share-of-wallet, share-of-competitor, regional capture (Denair / Hilmar / Hughson / Keyes / Delhi), and replacement demand** — not new arrivals.
2. **Recovery story, not a boom.** The dominant local fact is the climb out of **~17% unemployment (2010)**; the decade since is steady normalization, now decelerating to under 0.5% job growth.
3. **Real income barely moved** (~11–12% real over 13 years, ~0.9%/yr) → local purchasing power expanded slowly; demand growth is **population × stable real wages**, not rising affluence.
4. **The relevant macro risk is California-specific** (net domestic out-migration, cost structure), **not** Sunbelt-style — but the Central Valley is California's relative growth zone, making Turlock's near-flat reading notable even within CA.
5. **Benchmark honestly in owner-facing material:** do **not** position Turlock as a "growing Sunbelt-style market." Position it as a **stable Central Valley regional hub capturing surrounding small-town demand.**
6. **What macro can't resolve:** confirmed establishment counts and the **independent-vs-chain** split — that gap is the job of this project's business catalog, not macro data.
