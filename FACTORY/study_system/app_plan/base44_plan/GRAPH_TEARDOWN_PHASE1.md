## 1. Outcome

**Phase 2 set (Codex recommendation, endorsed):**
1. **TheBrain 15** — graph-first traversal: selecting a concept re-centers the network; focus-plus-context is the product. Free full local app, no account needed [doc — thebrain.com].
2. **Heptabase** — full learning content (cards/PDFs/media) lives inside the visual structure; nested topic boards. 7-day trial then $11.99/mo; account required [doc — heptabase.com].
3. **Kumu** — dense authored graphs tamed by focus-by-hop, dim-don't-remove ("showcase"), saved views, progressive disclosure. Free tier = PUBLIC projects only; account required [doc — kumu.io].

Fallback if only two: TheBrain + Heptabase. Runner-ups: Litmaps (saved-vs-recommended visual states, axis switching), Obsidian (the mature force-graph control baseline — and the documented cautionary tale that its GLOBAL graph is pretty but hard to navigate).

**Method validation:** the discovery step beat the seed list — TheBrain 15 and Litmaps were not seeds; seeds RemNote/XMind/MindNode were cut or out-ranked for our purpose (MindNode = polish benchmark but hierarchical-only, teaches little about a non-hierarchical graph). This is why DISCOVER precedes ANALYZE.

## 2. Load-bearing constraints captured in Phase 1

- **[doc — Heptabase support]** Heptabase's own docs warn whiteboards lag at ~100–150 cards. Our Slice 6 import is already 190 nodes → our density strategy MUST be focus/filter-first (neighborhood + dimming), never render-everything. Stress-test any borrowed density pattern; copy none blindly.
- **[doc — Kumu pricing]** Kumu free projects are public → Phase 2 hands-on there uses generic sample content only, never real study material.
- **[doc — Obsidian community]** Local (neighborhood) graph = used daily; global graph = admired, rarely used. Transfers directly: our default view should be a focused neighborhood, with the full graph as an explicit zoom-out act.
- **[SIGNAL — Codex synthesis]** Popular-list winners (XMind, MindMeister, Miro…) optimize fast creation/collaboration, not reading connected knowledge — mobile-store ratings were treated as supporting evidence only. Our purpose filter ("graph as a navigation-and-reading surface") is what produced this ranking.
