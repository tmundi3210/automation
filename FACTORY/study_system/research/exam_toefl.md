## 1. Current test structure (format effective January 21, 2026)

- [FACT] Four sections; single sitting; no scheduled breaks. Reading and Listening are **two-stage (multistage) adaptive**: everyone gets the same Stage 1; Stage 2 difficulty (and item count/time) adjusts to Stage 1 performance. Sources: ETS "TOEFL iBT Test Content, Structure and Administration" https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/about/content-structure.html (via search excerpt); ETS 2026 Test Blueprint & Specifications PDF https://www.eu.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-test-specifications-2026.pdf (existence [FACT]; contents relayed via third-party analyses, see §2).
- [FACT] ETS tells test takers to "allow approximately two hours"; as the test adapts, test time and items vary. Source: ETS content-structure page via search excerpt.
- [FACT] Working test time is roughly 1h23m–1h29m per blueprint-based analyses; widely marketed as "about 90 minutes." Sources: https://lingoleap.ai/toefl/2026-format , https://www.toeflmocktests.com/blog/toefl-exam-pattern-2026/ , https://toefl.magoosh.com/toefl-2026-changes (all third-party; consistent with each other).
- Per-section timing (third-party blueprint readings; treat as approximate):

| Section | Items (approx.) | Time (approx.) | Adaptive? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading | up to ~50 items, 35 raw points | ~18–30 min (varies by module) | Yes, 2-stage |
| Listening | up to ~47 items, 35 raw points | ~18–29 min (varies by module) | Yes, 2-stage |
| Speaking | 2 tasks (7 + 4 responses) | ~8 min | No |
| Writing | 3 tasks | ~23 min | No |

- [FACT] Scores reported within **72 hours or less** (down from 4–8 days). Source: ETS press release via search excerpt.
- [FACT] Content mix shifted from purely academic toward "modern, equitable" topics including daily-life English (emails, announcements, conversations). Source: ETS press release + content pages via search excerpts.

### Legacy format for reference (July 26, 2023 – January 20, 2026)

- [FACT] Introduced July 26, 2023: test under 2 hours (from ~3), no unscored questions, Reading cut to 2 passages/20 questions (~35 min), Listening 28 questions, Speaking unchanged (4 tasks), Writing = Integrated task + new **"Writing for an Academic Discussion"** task (replaced the Independent Essay). Sources: ETS press release https://www.ets.org/news/press-releases/toefl-ibt-enhancements-debuting-july-2023.html (via search excerpt); https://www.toeflresources.com/blog/toefl-changes-july-2023/ .
- [ESTIMATE — high confidence, widely published but not verified this session] Legacy per-section timing: Listening ~36 min, Speaking ~16 min, Writing ~29 min (Integrated 20 min + WAD 10 min).
- Writing for an Academic Discussion (WAD) — still exists in the 2026 test (see §2 Writing): student reads a professor's discussion-board prompt plus two classmate posts, writes their own contribution; legacy time 10 min, min. ~100 words recommended. [ESTIMATE — high confidence on task mechanics, standard published description; 10-min timing confirmed for 2026 version via §2 sources.]

## 2. Question types per section, enumerated (current 2026 test)

### Reading (3 task types)
| # | Task type | Format | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Complete the Words | Short paragraph (~70–100 words); 10 words have their second half deleted; type the missing letters; first sentence intact for context | Vocabulary knowledge + orthographic/lexical processing (form-focused reading) |
| R2 | Read in Daily Life | Short practical texts (~15–150 words): emails, texts, menus, bills, announcements; multiple choice | Comprehension of everyday functional English (A1–C2 range) |
| R3 | Read an Academic Passage | ~200-word academic passage (vs. ~700 legacy) + ~5 MC questions; disciplines: history, sciences, social sciences, humanities; no prior knowledge needed | Academic reading for main idea, detail, inference, vocabulary in context |

[FACT for the 3-type enumeration and mechanics] Sources: https://college-council.com/en/blog/toefl-2026-reading-new-tasks-strategies , https://lingoleap.ai/toefl/reading-question-types , https://prepdrills.com/blog/toefl-2026-reading-guide/ , https://testsucceed.com/materials/tests/toefl_new/en/samples/toefl-2026-new-reading-samples.html (all blueprint-derived third parties; consistent).

### Listening (4 task types)
| # | Task type | Format | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Listen and Choose a Response | ~8 short items: hear a line, pick the appropriate reply | Interactional listening / pragmatic response selection |
| L2 | Daily Life Conversation | Two-person dialogue (~90 sec) + ~5 questions | Main point, facts vs. opinions, agreement/disagreement, verbal cues |
| L3 | Daily Life Announcement | Short announcement (~90 sec) + ~5 questions | Extracting key information from public/service speech |
| L4 | Academic Talk | Short lecture-style talk (~90 sec) + ~5 questions | Academic listening: main idea, detail, speaker purpose |

[FACT for the 4-type enumeration] Sources: https://www.toeflresources.com/listening/ , https://www.writing30.com/blog/toefl-2026-listening-complete-guide , https://study.com/resources/master-new-toefl-listening.html , https://www.testglider.com/en/lessons/101/2026-TOEFL-Listening-Section . [ESTIMATE] Exact item counts per type vary by adaptive module.

### Speaking (2 tasks — replaced the legacy 4-task set)
| # | Task | Format | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Listen and Repeat | 7 sentences about campus/daily-life topics; hear each once, repeat aloud | Phonological/oral fluency, intelligibility, exact repetition accuracy |
| S2 | Take an Interview | Simulated interview with prerecorded interviewer; 4 questions on one topic, 45 sec each, **no prep time**; questions escalate from personal experience to broader opinion | Spontaneous speech: language use, organization, fluency, coherence |

[FACT] Sources: https://www.toeflresources.com/toefl-speaking-2026-changes-guide-and-samples/ , https://www.myspeakingscore.com/toefl-speaking-2026 , https://testsucceed.com/materials/tests/toefl_new/en/description/speaking/toefl-2026-new-take-an-interview.html , https://study.com/resources/new-toefl-speaking-practice-guide.html .

Legacy Speaking (2023–2025, for reading older prep material): Task 1 Independent (opinion, 15s prep/45s speak); Tasks 2–4 Integrated — T2 campus situation (read+listen+speak), T3 academic concept (read+listen+speak), T4 academic lecture summary (listen+speak), 30s prep/60s speak. [ESTIMATE — high confidence; standard published description, not re-verified this session.]

### Writing (3 tasks — replaced Integrated Essay + WAD pair)
| # | Task | Format | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Build a Sentence | Syntax drill: assemble/complete sentences | Grammatical accuracy, word order, syntax control |
| W2 | Write an Email | 7 minutes; write an email for a given scenario | Practical written communication, register, social/discourse conventions |
| W3 | Writing for an Academic Discussion | 10 minutes; contribute a post to a class discussion board (professor prompt + peer posts) | Reasoned written argument: relevance, elaboration, syntax, vocabulary |

[FACT] Sources: https://college-council.com/en/blog/toefl-2026-writing-new-tasks-guide , https://www.testglider.com/toefl/en/toefl-2026-update , https://toefl.magoosh.com/toefl-2026-changes . W3 is carried over from the July-2023 format.

## 3. Scoring

### Current scale (from Jan 21, 2026)
- [FACT] Each section scored **1.0–6.0 in 0.5-band increments**; overall = **average of the four sections, rounded to the nearest half band** (ETS example: average 5.125 → overall 5). Sources: ETS https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/score-scale-update.html and https://www.ets.org/toefl/china/toefl/score-scale-update.html via search excerpts.
- [FACT] Bands are **CEFR-aligned**; score reports carry a CEFR label (A1–C2) for overall and sections. Source: ETS score-scale-update page via search excerpt.
- [FACT] **Transition dual reporting (Jan 2026 – ~Jan 2028)**: reports show (1) CEFR level, (2) 1–6 band scores, (3) a comparable 0–120 overall score. ETS example: band 4.5 reported alongside an estimated 90 on the 0–120 scale. Source: ETS score-scale-update page via search excerpt.
- [ESTIMATE — medium confidence, third-party conversion anchors] band 4.0 ≈ B2 ≈ legacy 72+; band 4.5 ≈ ~90–102; band 5.0 ≈ C1 ≈ legacy 95+. Sources: https://study.com/toefl/scores/what-do-your-2026-toefl-scores-mean.html , https://www.toeflmocktests.com/scoring/ . The authoritative table is on the ETS score-scale-update page [UNKNOWN — could not fetch the exact full table].
- [FACT] Reading/Listening adaptive scoring reflects demonstrated proficiency level, not raw count; both sections carry 35 raw points feeding the band estimate. Sources: study.com/toeflmocktests blueprint analyses above.

### Legacy scale (still cited by most institutions through the transition)
- [FACT] Sections 0–30 each; total 0–120 (sum). Each section's range divided into 4–5 named proficiency levels (e.g., Reading/Listening "Advanced" ≈ 24–30, "High-Intermediate," "Low-Intermediate," "Below Low-Intermediate"). Official descriptor doc: https://www.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-performance-descriptors.pdf and https://toeflaccess.ets.org/help/en/articles/10388719-performance-descriptors-for-the-toefl-ibt-test (existence [FACT] via search; full level cutoffs per section [UNKNOWN — PDF not fetchable from sandbox]).
- [FACT] **MyBest scores (superscores)**: every score report includes the combination of the test taker's highest section scores from all valid TOEFL iBT dates in the **last 2 years**; MyBest total = sum of best sections (ETS worked example totals 90). Sources: ETS https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/understand-scores.html and https://www.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/mybest-scores-rationale-for-toefl-ibt-superscores.pdf via search excerpts. [UNKNOWN] Exactly how MyBest is computed on the 1–6 scale post-2026 (third parties say best sections re-averaged; not ETS-verified here).
- [FACT] Scores valid 2 years. Source: ETS understand-scores page via search excerpt.

## 6. From-zero learning ladder

### CEFR ↔ TOEFL mapping
- [FACT] The 2026 scale is CEFR-aligned **by design**: ETS publishes a chart comparing the 1–6 scale (total and sections) with CEFR levels and recommends institutions start from it. https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/score-scale-update.html (via search excerpt; full cell-by-cell table [UNKNOWN]).
- Working anchors (triangulated, tag per row):

| CEFR | New band (approx.) | Legacy 0–120 (approx.) | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | ~3.0–3.5 | ~42–71 | [ESTIMATE — medium; legacy B1=42–71 is ETS's published legacy mapping per long-standing compare-scores page https://www.ets.org/toefl/score-users (unreachable); band mapping inferred] |

- [FACT] During transition, ETS itself reports band 4.5 ≈ 90 (0–120). Source: ETS score-scale-update via search excerpt.

### Vocabulary size targets (citable research, not ETS)
- [FACT] Milton & Alexiou (2009), lexical-size study across 500+ learners: **A2 ≈ 1,500–2,500 words; B1 ≈ 2,750–3,250; B2 ≈ 3,250–3,750** (XLex-measured, first-5K band); vocabulary size explains ~60–70% of CEFR-level variance. Sources: https://eurosla.org/monographs/EM01/211-232Milton.pdf ; https://www.researchgate.net/publication/312063998_Vocabulary_size_and_the_common_European_framework_of_reference_for_languages
- [ESTIMATE — medium confidence] For comfortable authentic academic reading, research (Nation and successors) commonly cites 8,000–9,000 word families; not required for TOEFL band 4. Not re-verified this session.

### Beginner → TOEFL ladder (synthesis; [ESTIMATE] unless tagged)
1. **A0→A2 foundation**: ~2,000 highest-frequency words (Milton & Alexiou anchor above [FACT]); basic sentence patterns (SVO, questions, past/present/future); phonics + listen-and-repeat drills — directly feeds 2026 tasks R1 (Complete the Words) and S1 (Listen and Repeat), which reach down to A1 difficulty [FACT that the 2026 test measures down to A1 — blueprint-derived sources in §2].
2. **A2→B1**: everyday functional English — emails, announcements, menus, short dialogues; this is literally the 2026 task content (R2, L2, L3, W2). Target ≈ band 3–3.5.
3. **B1→B2 (typical university minimum ≈ band 4.0)**: short academic passages (~200 words), 90-second academic talks, timed 10-minute discussion posts (W3), 45-second spontaneous answers (S2). Vocabulary to ~3,500+ [FACT anchor above].
4. **B2→C1 (competitive programs ≈ band 5.0)**: speed + accuracy under adaptive pressure; upper-module Reading/Listening items reach C1/C2 [FACT — blueprint-derived sources §2].
5. Throughout: use TestReady daily free activity → free Jan-2026 sample test → paid TestReady section/full tests → Official Guide (from Aug 2026) as the mock-exam spine (§5).
