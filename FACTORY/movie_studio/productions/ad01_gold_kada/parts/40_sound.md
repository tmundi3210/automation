The script lives in `00_master_plan.md §5` (6 spoken lines; every authenticity fact is an `[OWNER-SLOT]` the seller fills with a TRUE value — an empty slot means the line is **cut, never guessed**). This section directs **how** it is spoken, not what it claims.

**Delivery direction:**

- **Warmth / tone:** warm, close, chest-resonant; a faint smile in the voice on the reveal and CTA; zero brightness/sibilance push. De-essed and gently compressed so it sits *in* the room the score builds, not on top of it.
- **Pauses (the load-bearing craft):** a **beat of silence before the sparkle payoff** ("Look at the work…" — let the eye catch the flare first), and a **held pause before the authenticity line** so the owner-verified fact lands deliberately, not glibly. - **Question-answer grammar:** where a line poses ("This is what real gold looks like."), let the **score answer** it (the piano/cello resolves into the next phrase) — the spoken line poses, the music resolves. [ESTIMATE — cross-media application of Zimmer's question-answer theme grammar]

## 3. SOUND-DESIGN SPOT LIST (synced to the cuts)

**Bar / cut grid.** [ESTIMATE — bar-math convention] At the Card D feel, pick a BPM whose bar (or half-bar) length lands on the cut frames. The clip grid is **4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 / 3 s**. A workable choice is **~66.7 BPM → 1 beat = 0.9 s, a 4-beat bar = 3.6 s**; simplest for cut-locking is to treat the **half-bar / 2-beat unit ≈ 1.8 s** and place transition SFX and the music's chord changes **on the cut frames at 4.0 / 8.0 / 12.0 / 15.0 / 19.0 s** rather than forcing a rigid bar count onto uneven 3 s and 4 s clips. **Edit to the music: source/generate the bed first, derive its grid, and move the cut to the music's event** — current AI music tools are strong at loopable beds and weak at frame-exact hit points. [FACT — conflict-resolution rule]

**Five-layer stack** (bed → spot FX → transition SFX → music → voice). Levels are craft convention: ambience bed **-25 to -35 dB under music**; VO ducks music **3–6 dB**; spot FX sit **near-music level** in this heritage card because they ARE the content. [ESTIMATE]

**Ambience bed:** one continuous, near-silent **warm interior room-tone** (a still, expensive room — no HVAC hiss, no traffic) running unbroken under all 6 clips at -28 to -32 dB. It is the glue that makes 6 stitched clips read as one place. A bed change would signal a scene change — there is none here, so the bed **never** changes. [ESTIMATE]

There is **no standalone music-generation lane** on this account. [FACT — live MCP surface enumeration, 2026-07-03]

### 4.1 How to get the VO
Two acceptable lanes; both are legitimate.
- **Lane 1 — Higgsfield speech (default).** [FACT — surface enumeration]
  - **`seed_audio`** — format `wav`/`mp3`/`pcm`/`ogg_opus`; **`sample_rate` = 48000** (use 48 kHz for delivery grade, **not** the 24000 default); `speech_rate`/`loudness_rate` -50..100 (dial pace **down** for the unhurried read); `pitch_rate` -12..12 (keep near 0 — a warm low-mid male, not artificially deepened); `voice_type` preset/element.
  - **or `text2speech_v2`** — **required** `variant` (one of `elevenlabs` / `minimax` / `seed_speech` / `vibe_voice` / `cozy_voice`).
  - Generate **dry**; do de-essing/compression/pacing in the edit.
- **Lane 2 — Recorded human VO.** Fully acceptable and often warmer for a heritage read; record dry at 48 kHz, then mix identically.
- **`create_voice` note / LIKENESS HARD STOP:** using a voice-creation/cloning path to **clone or evoke a specific real person's voice** is a **closed lane — refused, not optimized.** Any such request routes to the psych/likeness specialist's stop-checklist. A **generic, original preset/created brand voice** for the fictional presenter is fine; a **specific real person's voice** is not. [FACT — likeness boundary in both KBs]
- **Mix seat:** VO sits **dry, de-essed, compressed, on top**; music **side-chain ducked 3–6 dB** under it; verify intelligibility on a **mono phone-speaker** check. [ESTIMATE]

## 5. DELIVERY NOTE (mono-safe, ~-14 LUFS, gate)

**Mono-safe (the dominant constraint).** Reels default to **phone-speaker playback — effectively mono**, and platform re-encodes can downmix. **Fold the final mix to mono and check for phase cancellation** with **bass and lead elements centered.** The **VO, the cello, the CTA settle's thump, and every load-bearing SFX must survive the mono fold-down** — nothing critical lives only in a stereo side channel. Any width/binaural impression is a **headphone-only bonus over the mono baseline, never load-bearing.** No Atmos/spatial lane exists on Reels. [FACT — playback reality / FACT-shaped absence for no-Atmos]

**Loudness & format (stated as convention, NOT Meta spec):**
- Master to **≈ -14 LUFS integrated / -1 dBTP true peak** — a community/vendor **convention** (matches YouTube/TikTok practice). **Instagram publishes NO official loudness-normalization spec;** do not state -14 LUFS as a Meta requirement. [ESTIMATE — convention]
- **Upload defensively:** **AAC 256–320 kbps, 48 kHz, stereo, MP4/MOV** — so the platform's own transcode (reported ~128 kbps AAC-LC, an **[ESTIMATE]** because Instagram's engineering post was 403-blocked) starts from headroom. Do not state the transcode bitrate as platform fact. [ESTIMATE]
- **-1 dBTP headroom** prevents true-peak clipping in the AAC re-encode; louder-is-better buys nothing here. Verify by ear **on an actual phone after publish.**

**Delivery go/no-go gate (withhold "go" on any unmet item):**
- [ ] Mono fold-down: no disappearing/phase-cancelling element; bass + leads centered.
- [ ] VO intelligible on a mono phone speaker with music ducked 3–6 dB.
- [ ] Loudness ≈ -14 LUFS int, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP; target tagged as **convention**.
- [ ] Upload AAC 256–320 kbps / 48 kHz / stereo / MP4/MOV.
- [ ] Every transition SFX lands on its named cut frame (4.0/8.0/12.0/15.0/19.0 s); chime on the flare; settle on the CTA frame.
- [ ] Music source **strike-tier recorded**; **no Tier-4**; if cross-platform posting is intended, source is a **paid cross-platform library**, not Meta Sound Collection.
- [ ] Higgsfield commercial-terms for any off-Meta use **read from ToS**, not assumed.
- [ ] **No real-person voice** cloned/evoked; presenter voice is an original/generic brand voice.
- [ ] Reel exported **watermark-free** (leftover tool watermarks suppress reach — carried from master plan §7).
