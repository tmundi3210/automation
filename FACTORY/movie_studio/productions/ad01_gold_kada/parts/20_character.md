## 2. CONSISTENCY PLAN — carrying identity across the 6 shots

### 2.1 The core routing decision: **Reference-Element lane, not the Soul lane**
There are **two** reference identities this ad must hold in the same frames: **(a) the presenter's face/forearm** and **(b) the exact product bracelet** (owner's uploaded photo). The platform imposes a hard constraint:

- **Reference Elements are NOT usable with the Soul models** (`soul_2` / `soul_cinematic`), and only **one `soul_id` per generation** is allowed. `[FACT — live surface]`
- The **product must** be an **image reference** (owner's photo) — text cannot carry the exact engraving/terminal motif. An image reference of a prop is an **Element / start_image**, which **cannot co-exist with a Soul in one call.**

**Therefore any shot that contains the presenter *and* the product on-body cannot run on the Soul lane.** The whole production routes through the **Reference-Element lane** for a single coherent carrier family:

- **Presenter = one `character` Reference Element** (`<<<aurelio_face>>>`), built from a curated on-identity set (§2.2). `[ESTIMATE]`
- **Product = one `prop` Reference Element** (`<<<kada_ref>>>`) created from the **owner's uploaded kada photo at run time**. `[FACT — owner upload via widget]`
- Presenter-with-product shots invoke **both tokens in one prompt** (multi-Element is the surface-designated multi-reference path). No Soul is used, so the "never mix Soul + Element" rule is honoured by construction. `[FACT]`

**Soul-vs-Element approach — the honest trade:** a trained **Soul** is the stronger face-fidelity carrier for a *solo* hero on a Soul-compatible model, and would be the textbook choice for shots 2/5/6 **if the product were not in frame**. It is **not usable here** because the product Element must share the frame. The **fidelity gap between Soul and Element is `[UNKNOWN]`** and is not assumed away — it is resolved by QC on the first batch (§2.5), and by an optional early cheap A/B if the owner wants the Soul-vs-Element gap priced. **Recommendation: Reference-Element lane as primary.** `[ESTIMATE]`

### 2.5 Drift QC — taxonomy, embedding gate, per-shot checklist
Every generated still is gated **before** it seeds a (more expensive) video clip. Classify any reject against the **named drift taxonomy** so failures are diagnosable, not just flagged. `[FACT — drift taxonomy]`

**Two-part gate, both run on every batch:**
1. **Embedding-similarity check** — an ArcFace-class embedding of each kept generation, cosine-compared to the **canonical embedding** built from the `<<<aurelio_face>>>` curation set. The **accept threshold is `[UNKNOWN]`** for a synthetic face — **calibrate it on this program's own first accepted/rejected batch**; do not assume a number. `[FACT on mechanism; threshold UNKNOWN]`
2. **Human consistency checklist** — face vs the bible/turnaround; apparent age within the 42 band; skin tone in the **lit** scene; all five hair slots + left part; amber-brown deep-set eyes + brows; jaw/cheekbone silhouette in ¾ view; §1.5 marks; single stubble state; wrist = right; **product motif/texture/rails faithful and un-garbled**; no hallmark; no chain.

**Escalation (not resolved in this part):** a disputed *is-it-real-enough / is-the-face-drifted* verdict goes to the **realism specialist** (hard reviewer of record) after these gates run; any **likeness-convergence** signal goes to the **psych/likeness specialist** and **stops** work, not "adjust and continue"; platform/policy questions go to the **media/platform specialist**.

## 3. WARDROBE — quiet luxury that flatters yellow gold and frames the kada

### 3.1 Palette & fabric — engineered to make warm 22k-look gold sing
Warm yellow gold reads richest against **warm neutrals and earth tones**; cold, icy or high-shine surfaces fight it. `[ESTIMATE — from quiet-luxury codes]`
- **Colours (persona palette for this ad):** camel · oatmeal/ivory · warm charcoal · deep espresso brown · tobacco · soft olive. Deep **navy** is permitted **only as an anchor jacket paired with a warm shirt** (per master-plan §6), never head-to-toe cool.
- **Avoid:** stark cool white, icy blue-grey, cold jet-black, and anything **high-shine/synthetic** — a specular fabric competes with the gold's diamond-cut flare (the whole hook).
- **Fabrics (quiet-luxury, no logos, no synthetics):** brushed cashmere, fine merino, garment-washed cotton/poplin, raw silk, suede, warm-weather linen. **Matte, tactile** — the only thing allowed to sparkle is the kada. `[FACT — menswear codes]`
- **No visible logo, monogram or brand mark** anywhere (no-logo philosophy). Reference houses (Zegna, Loro Piana, Brunello Cucinelli, The Row, Savile Row cut) are **aesthetic calibration only** — they translate into fabric/fit words, never names. `[FACT]`

### 3.2 Cuff & sleeve engineering — how the cloth frames a wrist-worn kada
The single most product-relevant wardrobe decision. The kada sits on **bare right wrist**; cloth must **reveal, not crowd** it.
- **Cuff breaks just above the wrist bone**, leaving the kada fully on **bare skin** — a shirt barrel cuff that stops ~1 cm short, worn **open or turned one roll**, or a jacket sleeve showing a short reveal of shirt linen. `[ESTIMATE]`
- For the **forearm hero and macros**, the sleeve is **pushed to mid-forearm** on a fine knit — bare wrist, gold against warm skin.
- **No cufflinks near the kada** (a second metal competes with the gold) and **no busy pattern** at the wrist — the fabric adjacent to the kada is a **warm solid** that sets the gold off.
- Keep the cuff **loose enough** that it never overlaps or hides the band.

### 3.3 Watch & other metal — protect the "single gold note"
- **Recommended: no wristwatch at all**, so nothing shares focus with the kada. `[ESTIMATE]`
- If the owner wants a watch, it goes on the **left (opposite) wrist only**, and is a **slim, minimalist piece on a warm brown leather strap** (warm/champagne-toned dial) — **never** a large steel/sports watch, and never on the kada wrist. The kada remains the **only gold flex.** `[FACT — master plan §6]`
- Opposite wrist and hands otherwise **bare of jewelry** (a plain warm band is optional but discouraged — it competes). No chain is worn or implied.

### 3.4 The hero module (one look, carried across all presenter shots)
For a 22-second reel that must read as **one man in one world**, the presenter wears **one wardrobe module** across every clip he appears in; only the **cuff/sleeve state** changes by framing. This is both quiet-luxury discipline and continuity insurance.

> **Module `M-KADA-A` (formality band 3 — smart-casual/refined, warm-anchor):** an **unstructured warm-charcoal (or deep-navy) brushed-cashmere jacket**, worn open, over a **fine oatmeal/ivory open-collar shirt** (top button undone, no tie), in matte warm-neutral tones; **no logos, no cufflinks, no visible watch on the right wrist**; the **kada on the bare right wrist** is the single gold note. Paste this descriptor **verbatim** in every presenter prompt, directly after the §1.2 identity block. `[ESTIMATE — owner sign-off]`

*Band note:* this is a band-3 module; the ad has no gala/black-tie beat, so no Debrett's black-tie spec is needed here. If the owner ever requests a formal cut, that becomes a new **band-4/5 module** with the black-tie correctness set (peaked/shawl lapel, covered buttons, no vents…), logged separately — out of scope for `ad01`.
