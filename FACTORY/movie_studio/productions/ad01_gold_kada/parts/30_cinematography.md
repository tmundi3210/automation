## 0. The floor, fixed before any shot (lighting)

**Motivated-lighting veto [FACT def / ESTIMATE anti-AI linkage].** Every visible light in every prompt must imitate a plausible in-world source (a window, a warm lamp/practical, a soft overhead panel) even when the fixture is off-screen. If a frame carries a glow no named source explains, it is regenerated — unmotivated glow is the AI tell.

**Contrast register [FACT].** The whole reel defaults to **controlled contrast (moderate low-key)**, not flat high-key. Flat high-key sits next to the plastic-render look, which is fatal for a premium gold-plated product. Macros run darkest (clean gradient falling to near-black surround); presenter beats run soft-warm low-key with a lifted-but-sculpted face.

**The reflection principle is the spine of every gold frame [ESTIMATE].** For polished 24k-gold-plated yellow you **light the reflections, not the metal**: place one or two *very large soft warm sources* so the band reflects a clean bright gradient, and put **black negative fill** where the metal needs something dark to reflect. The gradient + the dark give the diamond-cut cross-hatch its contrast to sparkle against. Direct hard light on the gold blows it into a featureless plastic blob — the opposite of the sell.

**Anti-AI floor, appended to every prompt [ESTIMATE — effectiveness untested until credit run].** Persona beats: `"natural motivated lighting, realistic skin texture, film grain, no artificial smoothness."` Gold macros (skin-texture clause swapped for metal): `"natural motivated lighting, realistic engraved metal micro-texture, fine diamond-cut facets, film grain, no artificial smoothness, no plastic CGI gold."` Plus **exactly one living micro-detail** per shot (named per shot below).

**One recipe per shot [FACT].** No prompt stacks two lighting ideas — an extra idea means another shot, not a longer prompt. The recipe leads the prompt clause order (lighting is the single most impactful lever, per the Veo excerpt [FACT for Veo; working assumption elsewhere]).

### CLIP 1 — HOOK · `ad01_s1_hook` · 4.0 s · tight forearm/wrist

**LIGHTING — warm low-key with one controlled traveling specular (hybrid: diffuse skin + reflection-lit gold) [ESTIMATE on the gold half].**
- Recipe (leads prompt): *warm low-key interior light; a single large soft warm source rakes across the forearm, motivated as low warm window/lamp light; the high-polish gold kada catches one clean traveling specular that flares into diamond-cut sparkle as the wrist turns; deep shadow surround (black negative fill) so the band reads bright against dark; tailored unbranded dark sleeve.*
- Skin takes soft **direct** warm key (diffuse subject); the gold takes the **reflection** treatment (large soft source reflected as a gradient, not a hot point).

**CAMERA MOVE — static-locked lens; slow subject-driven forearm rotation → rapid whip-pan right at the tail.**
- **Emotional reason:** composed confidence + swipe-stop. The locked frame makes the diamond-cut *flare* the single hero event (motion in frame one is the wrist, not the camera — cheapest coherence, strongest read). The tail **rapid whip-pan** injects the kinetic energy that carries the hook into the reveal and forgives the seam. Speed: interior slow, seam whip **rapid**.

### CLIP 3 — QUALITY MACRO (BAND) · `ad01_s3_band_macro` · 4.0 s · extreme macro

**LIGHTING — reflection lighting, hero application [ESTIMATE — still-validate before hero spend].**
- Recipe: *reflection lighting — one or two very large soft warm sources create a clean bright gradient down the two polished rails; black negative fill above and below so the rails read as bright edges against dark and the engraved cross-hatch throws its diamond-cut shimmer; one specular that travels along the band as the camera glides; clean warm-to-dark gradient background; shallow depth of field.*
- The **black surround is the negative fill** — it is what gives the gold something dark to reflect and what makes the wheat-grain sparkle pop. Guard the gradient highlight: shadow may take everything except the rails' bright edge and the sparkle.
- **Living micro-detail:** the traveling specular roll (the sparkle animation) — no hands in frame.
- **Hard rule:** generate **no hallmark, karat stamp or maker's mark** — keep the macro on the plain textured band only (authenticity marks are owner facts, not lit invention).

**CAMERA MOVE — slow lateral travel along the band (tracking macro), slow.**
- **Emotional reason:** the appraisal of craftsmanship — the glide + the traveling specular animate the diamond-cut sparkle beat by beat. Lateral travel is normally penalized in 9:16 [FACT], but here it *earns its pixels*: the band is a horizontal line filling the frame, so the passing content is the subject itself, not a wasted environment. Tail: focus begins easing off the band toward the terminal (rack setup for Seam 3).

**CAMERA MOVE — rack focus settling onto the terminal motif, slow.**
- **Emotional reason:** redirect the eye to the signature "made-to-last" detail *without a cut* — the focus landing on the temple/Greek-key panel is the whole beat. Rack focus needs a visibly shallow DoF to register [FACT] — carried from Clip 3's shallow macro DoF. Tail: **rapid whip-pan out** back toward the presenter (Seam 4).

## 2. TRANSITIONS — 5 seams as END-frame + START-frame constraint pairs

Seams are chosen **first**; both boundary frames are written as **two still keyframes** sharing every continuity match variable — *texture, color, brightness, lighting-recipe string (byte-identical), lens language* — before any video is generated [FACT / ESTIMATE frame-pair discipline]. Each seam routes only to a **dual-keyframe model** that declares both `start_image` and `end_image` [FACT].

**Continuity law [FACT]:** at every boundary the lighting-recipe string is copied **byte-identical** into end-N and start-N+1. Per-clip lighting optimization is allowed only in a clip's *interior*, never at its boundary frames.

**Surface limit [FACT / UNKNOWN]:** no video schema exposes a camera-move parameter, so all whip / ramp / match language rides in **prompt text, presets, the `cinematic_studio_video_v2` speedramp param, or motion_control**. ### SEAM 2 · Clip 2 → Clip 3 · **MATCH CUT ON THE GOLD** (precision — HERO seam)

## 4. Handoff to REALISM QC (sparkle + gold-accuracy — the flagged calls)

Cine applies the anti-AI floor, the five-check lighting gate and the stills-match gate, then **escalates the disputed is-it-real-enough call to the realism specialist** (hard reviewer of record). The following are fed forward as gating checks on the **stills, before any hero video spend**:

1. **Gold color accuracy.** Warm 24k-gold-plated yellow — reject brassy/greenish/pale-plastic or CGI-chrome renders. The reflection recipe is [ESTIMATE]; it must earn a passing validation still before hero video credits.
2. **Sparkle authenticity.** The diamond-cut shimmer must read as **engraved cross-hatch / wheat-grain facets catching light**, not applied glitter or noise. The traveling specular is the intended mechanism — QC that it reads as cut metal.
3. **Rails + band structure.** The two polished rails must stay bright, clean and parallel; the cross-hatch texture must not smear into mush at macro scale.
4. **Terminal motif non-garble (worst-artifact gate).** The temple / Greek-key openwork must be symmetric and legible — any garbled or asymmetric motif forces a **re-roll, not a ship**. Primary mitigation: the exact piece rides the **owner's reference photo**.
5. **No fabricated authenticity marks.** No hallmark, karat stamp or maker's mark generated anywhere in the macros; the lower-third authenticity text is composited and only ever populated with **true [OWNER-SLOT] values**.

**Blocking asset dependency (carried from the master plan):** the exact bracelet is carried by **image reference** — remote generation needs the owner to upload the Kada photo through the **Higgsfield media-upload widget at run time** (the connector cannot read a chat attachment). **No chain shot exists in this plan** — a chain is an OWNER-SLOT needing a reference image first; no chain lighting/move/seam is specified.

## 5. Five-check lighting QC gate (run + logged on every accepted still/clip) [FACT components / ESTIMATE process]

1. **Motivation** — every visible light justified by a named in-world source (window / warm practical / soft panel). No unexplained glow.
2. **Recipe match** — the frame followed its declared signature (reflection-lit gold macros; window-light quiet-luxury presenter).
3. **Floor present** — anti-AI floor clause + one living micro-detail in the prompt.
4. **Seam-pair match** — boundary frames byte-identical on the lighting-recipe string and matched on brightness/color/blur direction.
5. **Honesty log** — evidence tags accurate; the reflection-lighting/jewelry-transfer [ESTIMATE] tag never silently upgraded, and carried into every downstream brief with its still-validation flag.
