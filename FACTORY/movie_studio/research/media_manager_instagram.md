## 2. Format strategy: Reels vs carousels vs stories — what Instagram has actually said

### 2.1 Official ranking statements
- Instagram's official "Instagram Ranking Explained" post (about.instagram.com) states there is **no single algorithm**: Feed, Stories, Explore, and Reels each have their own ranking system tuned to how people use that surface. [FACT-excerpt — official page (1) proxy-blocked; consistently reported by (2),(3)]
- Adam Mosseri (Jan 2025 statements, widely reported): the three signals that matter most for reach are **watch time, likes per reach, and sends (DM shares) per reach**; sends per reach is the strongest signal for reaching NON-followers, likes per reach matters more for follower ("connected") reach. [FACT-excerpt — Mosseri video statements reported by (2),(3),(4); primary video on instagram.com not fetchable]
- Ratio framing matters: these are per-reach ratios, not raw counts. [FACT-excerpt — same sources]

### 2.2 Format performance (third-party, not official)
- Reels get the highest average reach rate of any format (Socialinsider 2026 benchmark reported ~30.8% avg reach rate, roughly 2–3× carousels); carousels lead on engagement rate (~0.55% vs ~0.50–0.52% for Reels, a lead held since 2023). [ESTIMATE — Socialinsider benchmark as relayed by (5),(6); aggregate averages across industries; medium confidence, not Meta data]
- Working rule derived: **Reels = discovery engine (non-follower reach), carousels = depth/saves engine (follower nurture), Stories = retention/relationship surface (followers only, ranked by closeness).** The Stories-ranked-by-relationship point is from Instagram's own ranking explainer. [FACT-excerpt for Stories ranking (1),(2); ESTIMATE for the strategic division of labor]
- Anti-folklore note: claims like "Instagram punishes accounts that post too many Reels per day" or fixed "engagement windows in the first 30 minutes" have **no located official source**; treat as folklore. [UNKNOWN — no official statement found; do not encode as rules]

### 2.3 What Instagram says does NOT drive reach
- Mosseri (Feb 2024 and May 2024, repeated since): hashtags **do not meaningfully increase reach**; they help categorize/search. [FACT-excerpt — reported by (7),(8),(9)]
- Visible third-party watermarks (e.g. TikTok logos) on Reels make content less likely to be recommended — stated in Instagram's official Reels recommendation guidance and widely reported; relevant because the pipeline must export clean, watermark-free renders from Higgsfield. [ESTIMATE — widely reported official guidance; official page not fetched this session; medium-high confidence]

## 3. Posting cadence and timing — the honest state of evidence

### 3.1 Platform statements (highest authority)
- Mosseri's stated baseline for growth: **a couple of feed posts per week + 1–2 Stories per day**, with the explicit caveat that the optimal schedule is the one you can sustain: "I'd rather you post twice a week for two years than every day for two months and then quit." [FACT-excerpt — Mosseri statements reported by (10),(11)]
- No official Instagram statement prescribes a best clock time to post. [FACT-excerpt — absence confirmed across (10),(12),(13); no official timing claim located]

### 3.2 Third-party timing studies (all [ESTIMATE])
- Aggregate 2025–2026 studies (Buffer ~9.6M posts, Sprout Social, Later, Hootsuite; combined >19M posts) converge loosely on: midweek (Wed/Thu strongest), late-morning-to-early-evening windows (≈11:00–19:00 audience local time), 11:00–13:00 the most-repeated single window; Sprout/Hootsuite also flag 19:00–21:00; early morning (<08:00) and post-22:00, especially 00:00–04:00, weakest; Fri/Sat weakest days in several datasets. [ESTIMATE — third-party aggregate studies (12),(13),(14); these are cross-industry averages, systematically biased toward US/EU business audiences; low-medium confidence for THIS account's audience]
- Honest hierarchy for the media manager: (1) consistency and per-post quality (platform-stated), (2) your OWN Insights "most active times" once ≥2–4 weeks of data exist, (3) aggregate study windows only as a cold-start default. [ESTIMATE — synthesis; high confidence in the ordering, since ratio signals in §2.1 are content-quality signals, not clock signals]

### 3.3 Cold-start cadence recommendation
- Weeks 1–4: 4–5 Reels/week, 1 carousel/week, 1–2 Stories/day, posted in the 11:00–13:00 or 19:00–21:00 target-audience windows; revisit after the first Insights cycle (§6). [ESTIMATE — recommendation combining §3.1 platform baseline (upweighted for a from-zero Reels-led account) with §3.2 defaults; medium confidence]

## 4. Motivational-quote video conventions

### 4.1 Text-on-video safe zones (Reels, 1080×1920)
- Instagram UI overlays occupy roughly: top ~200 px (username/follow), bottom ~320–420 px (caption, audio attribution), right ~120–150 px (like/comment/share rail). Practitioner safe-zone consensus: keep critical text within a central region ≈ 1080-wide minus ~60 px left / ~120 px right, and vertically between ~108–200 px from top and ~320–420 px from bottom; several guides simplify to "keep text in the central 70–80% of frame height." [ESTIMATE — third-party safe-zone guides (18),(19); pixel figures vary ±40 px between guides because Meta publishes exact ad-placement zones but not a single organic-Reels spec; medium confidence. Official Meta spec page: proxy-blocked, unverified]
- Also design for the 4:5 (1080×1350) feed crop: the feed preview center-crops the 9:16 frame, so the quote text must survive a 4:5 center crop. [ESTIMATE — standard practice per (18); high confidence mechanically]

### 4.2 Hook-line craft
- Watch time is the top-weighted signal (§2.1), so the first 1–3 s carry the ranking load. Hook patterns for quote videos: (a) open mid-motion (car door, jet stair, wave crash) with the first line already on screen; (b) front-load the quote's most confrontational clause, not the attribution; (c) never open with a logo card or fade-from-black. [ESTIMATE — craft conventions derived from the watch-time fact; the specific patterns are practitioner convention, not measured]
- Text pacing: one thought per 1.5–2.5 s beat matches the owner's 3–6 s stitched-clip format — one quote line per clip, resolution line on the final clip. [ESTIMATE — design rule matched to OWNER_BRIEF.md §2b.14; untested]

### 4.3 Quote sourcing and attribution ethics
- Copyright: short phrases are generally not copyrightable, but longer passages are; **attribution does not substitute for permission** — you can credit an author perfectly and still infringe; commercial use raises the risk. [FACT-excerpt — legal-information sources (20),(21); high consistency across sources]
- Public domain: US works published before ~1930/1931 (95-year rule rolling) are public domain; authors dead >70 years are the common rule for life+70 jurisdictions. Safe default lane: classical/stoic/pre-1930 authors (Seneca, Marcus Aurelius in old translations — note: *specific modern translations are themselves copyrighted*), or original in-house lines written for the personas. [FACT-excerpt for the rules (20),(21); the translation caveat is standard copyright doctrine, high confidence]
- Misattribution ethics: attributing an invented or wrong quote to a real living/dead person is an integrity failure and a reputational/legal risk (false endorsement flavor); the pipeline rule: **every attributed quote must be verified to a primary source, else publish it unattributed as house writing.** [FACT-excerpt on the risk (21); the pipeline rule is a binding design decision]
- Living-author quotes (e.g. contemporary motivational speakers): highest-risk lane — potentially copyrighted AND right-of-publicity adjacent when paired with luxury persona imagery; default = avoid; use only with permission. [ESTIMATE — legal-prudence synthesis of (20),(21); high confidence in the direction]

1. **Filter**: is the item on-genre (classic-luxurious cars/places/craft) and evergreen for ≥1 week? Skip hard news, scandals, politics.

## 6. Analytics feedback loop

### 6.1 Metric ground truth (post-April 2025)
- Meta deprecated **Impressions, Plays, and Video Views** and consolidated on **"Views"** as the primary metric across posts, Reels, Stories, profiles, effective April 21, 2025 (announced Jan 8, 2025 via Meta developer blog; Mosseri signaled it Aug 2024). Views counts every play/display incl. repeats, so it runs higher than old Impressions (25%+ reported). [FACT-excerpt — API deprecation independently documented by tool vendors (25),(26),(27); official developer-blog page not fetched]
- Native metrics that map to the stated ranking signals (§2.1): **Views, Reach (unique), Watch time / avg watch time & retention curve, Likes, Sends (shares), Saves, Follows from the post, Non-follower % of reach, Profile visits.** [FACT-excerpt — Insights surface as documented by (25),(26); exact panel naming varies by app version — [UNKNOWN] exact current label set until read off the live account]
- Derived ratios the manager tracks (compute, not native): likes/reach, sends/reach, saves/reach, follows/reach, avg-watch-time ÷ video length (completion %). [ESTIMATE — direct operationalization of §2.1 facts; high confidence as a design]

### 6.2 Weekly review protocol (feeds the content plan AND the generation briefs)
[ESTIMATE — original protocol design, grounded in §2/§6.1 facts]:
1. Export per-post: format, pillar (quote/travel/car/fitness), persona (him/her/both), hook type, scenario-bank ID, posting slot, and the ratio set above.
2. Rank by **sends/reach** (non-follower growth) and **follows/reach** (conversion); rank separately by **avg watch time** (hook + edit quality) and **saves/reach** (depth).
3. Retention-curve read: a cliff at 0–3 s = hook/first-clip failure → revise hook conventions (§4.2) in the DIRECTOR brief; mid-video decay = pacing/transition failure → route to the editing/camera-tricks specialist; strong completion but weak sends = emotionally flat → route to story/psychologist specialists.
4. Decisions each week: kill/keep/double each pillar-persona-hook cell (minimum 3 posts per cell before judging — small-sample honesty); promote one winning Trial Reel pattern to the main cadence; update the news-category weights (§5.2.6).
5. Write-back: the weekly memo updates (a) next week's calendar, (b) the standing generation-brief defaults (hook type, clip length distribution, grade, score mood) — this is the owner-required feedback loop into the movie pipeline. [FACT that the loop is required — OWNER_BRIEF.md §2b.11/12; the mechanism is ESTIMATE/design]
- Cold-start caveat: ratios are noisy under ~1k reach; first 2–3 weeks are calibration, not verdicts. [ESTIMATE — statistical common sense; high confidence]

## 7. Compliance (Meta/Instagram) — all items carry the §0 downgrade caveat

**Global caveat: every official Meta policy page attempted this session was proxy-blocked (403), and archive.org was blocked; nothing below could be read on a Meta domain first-hand. ### 7.1 AI-generated content labeling
- Since May 2024 Meta labels AI content with what is now the **"AI info"** label; labels are applied when Meta detects industry-standard AI indicators (e.g. C2PA-style metadata) **or when the poster self-discloses**. Meta **requires** disclosure for photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally generated or altered; the share screen has an add-AI-label toggle. [FACT-excerpt — official pages (28),(29) blocked; excerpts + (30),(31)]
- Meta has stated it may apply penalties for failing to disclose such content; secondary sources describe reduced distribution/removal for repeat failure. Exact penalty ladder: [UNKNOWN — official wording not readable this session; do not encode specifics]
- Meta has additionally rolled out an optional **"AI creator" profile label** creators can toggle on, applying to the profile and posts. [FACT-excerpt — reported by (31),(32)]
- Engagement impact of the label: one secondary analysis claims 15–30% engagement reduction for labeled photos; treat as **weak evidence** — methodology not inspected. [ESTIMATE — single secondary claim (30); low confidence]
- **Operational rule for this account: photoreal AI personas = always self-label. The account's content is squarely inside the mandatory-disclosure class (photorealistic generated video). Non-negotiable.** [Design decision compelled by the FACT-excerpt above]

### 7.2 Branded content / paid partnerships
- Branded content (content featuring/influenced by a business partner for payment or free goods) **must** be posted with the branded-content tool / **"Paid partnership with [brand]"** label; the brand must approve the tag and hold a professional account; available on Feed, Stories, Reels, Live, broadcast channels. Accounts flagged for misinformation can lose branded-content eligibility. [FACT-excerpt — official Help Center pages (24),(33) blocked; excerpts consistent across (24),(33),(34)]
- Also FTC territory in the US (material-connection disclosure) — outside this file's scope but flagged for the compliance layer. [ESTIMATE — well-established regulatory context; high confidence]

### 7.3 Community standards relevant to physique/swimwear content
- Meta's Adult Nudity and Sexual Activity policy prohibits nudity/explicit content; swimwear/fitness/physique content is generally allowed **but** Instagram's **Recommendation Guidelines** exclude "sexually suggestive" content from Explore/Reels recommendations even when it doesn't violate removal rules — it can still reach followers but loses non-follower distribution; **repeated violations can make the whole account ineligible for recommendations**. [FACT-excerpt — official pages (35),(36) blocked; excerpts + (37)]
- Direct consequence for the psychologist-specialist's "sexy figures" remit (OWNER_BRIEF §2b.13): the growth engine is non-follower recommendation (§2.1), so **content must stay on the aspirational-fitness/swimwear side, never the sexually-suggestive side, or the account's discovery pipeline silently dies.** Where the line sits exactly is enforced by classifiers Meta does not publish: [UNKNOWN — no published threshold; design conservatively and watch the "ineligible for recommendation" account-status screen in-app].

### 7.4 Impersonation
- Instagram prohibits accounts pretending to be another person; reports ("pretending to be someone else") can lead to post removal, warnings, or account removal, with ID verification in the process. [FACT-excerpt — official Help Center (38) blocked; excerpts + (39)]
- Application here: the two personas must be presented as **original (AI) personas**, not as any real individual; combined with 7.1 self-labeling and the "Behind the scenes (AI)" highlight (§1.3), impersonation risk is designed out. Celebrity-adjacent content boundary lives in the dedicated celebrity/right-of-publicity research file. [Design decision; boundary FACT per OWNER_BRIEF §2b.17]

## 8. Growth mechanics without fakery

### 8.1 Collab posts
- Instagram Collab posts let up to ~5 accounts co-author one post/Reel: it appears on all co-authors' grids and reaches all their audiences, with one shared engagement pool; available for feed posts and Reels, not Stories. [FACT-excerpt — official Help Center (40) blocked; excerpts + (41),(42)]
- Emplifi analysis (>1.1M posts) reports collab posts earn >2× the impressions/interactions of non-collab organic. [ESTIMATE — third-party dataset via (42); medium confidence]
- Use: the two-persona structure is a built-in collab engine if run as two accounts (§1.2); externally, collab with adjacent luxury/motivation pages once the account has trade-able reach. [ESTIMATE — strategy]

### 8.2 Trial Reels
- Officially launched Dec 10, 2024 (announced by Mosseri): a "Trial" toggle shows a Reel to **non-followers only**; it skips followers' feeds and the profile grid; ~24 h later Instagram surfaces comparative performance and can auto-share winners to everyone based on first-72-h views (opt-in); eligibility: public account, ≥1,000 followers; scheduling later added; ~5 trials/day limit reported. [FACT-excerpt — official creators.instagram.com post (16) blocked-class domain; excerpts + (17),(43)]
- Use: A/B lab for hook types, persona split, quote styles — feeds §6.2 step 4 without burning follower trust. Note the 1k-follower gate means weeks 1–N run without it. [ESTIMATE — strategy; the gate is FACT-excerpt]

### 8.3 Caption SEO and hashtags — current state
- Keywords in captions, bio, alt text, and on-screen/spoken text drive Instagram search and recommendations; hashtags categorize but do not boost reach (Mosseri, statements Feb 2024–2025, source dates as reported). [FACT-excerpt — (9),(15),(7); statement dates per secondary reporting]
- As of ~July 10, 2025: Instagram allows Google/Bing to index public content from professional accounts — captions are now web-SEO surface too. [FACT-excerpt — reported by (44),(45); official announcement page not fetched]
- Practical: write captions as searchable sentences ("luxury morning routine in Lake Como" not "#blessed"); ≤5 relevant hashtags as taxonomy, zero reach expectation. [ESTIMATE — direct operationalization; high confidence]

### 8.4 Remix
- Remix lets users create side-by-side/response Reels to public Reels; a discovery mechanic when remixing trending formats. [ESTIMATE — well-known feature; official page not fetched this session; mechanics unverified in detail — verify at setup]

### 8.5 Explicit no-go list (fakery)
Bought followers/engagement pods/bot comments: violate Meta authenticity policies, poison the §6 ratio signals the whole system steers by, and produce audiences that never convert. Banned in this operation. [ESTIMATE — policy direction well established; high confidence; also a pipeline-integrity decision]

## 9. 4-week starter calendar skeleton (maps to persona + scenario bank)

[ESTIMATE — original design; applies §3.3 cadence, §2 format split, §6 learning loop. Slots use cold-start windows pending own-audience data. "Him"/"Her" = the male/female persona; scenario IDs to be bound to the scenario-bank file.]

**Standing weekly rhythm (all 4 weeks):** 4 Reels + 1 carousel + 1–2 Stories/day (BTS frames, polls, countdowns). All photoreal posts self-labeled AI (§7.1).

- **Week 1 — Establish the contract (who are they, what does this feel like):**
  R1 Him, quote-over-drive reel (coastal road, dawn grade) · R2 Her, quote-over-arrival reel (grand hotel) · R3 Both, silent cinematic "world" reel (place-first, personas incidental) · R4 Him, fitness-accent discipline quote (gym-at-dawn, classic grade) · C1 carousel: 6-frame "manifesto" (grid-defining stills + house quote).
- **Week 2 — Persona depth + first news conversion:**
  R1 Her, story-beat reel (3-clip micro-narrative, no quote) · R2 news→reel via §5.2 template (auto/travel item of the week) · R3 Him, quote reel, new hook type (mid-motion vs text-first — record hook variant) · R4 Both, travel set-piece · C1 carousel: "the wardrobe" (capsule pieces as objects — feeds commerce-ready aesthetic).
- **Week 3 — First analytics-driven fork:**
  Run §6.2 on weeks 1–2. Double the best pillar-persona cell (2 of 4 Reels), keep 1 news-conversion slot, 1 deliberate experiment slot (weakest hook type, revised per retention curves) · C1 carousel: quote series (5 slides, save-bait).
- **Week 4 — Consolidate + widen:**
  Repeat winning cells; introduce 1 remix-format or trending-audio experiment (§8.4) if on-brand; C1 carousel: month-one recap grid; if ≥1k followers reached, begin Trial Reels program (§8.2) with 3 trials of next month's hook candidates; write month-one memo → BUILD phase brief defaults.
